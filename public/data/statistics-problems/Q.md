# SAT Prep: Statistics Problems - Practice Questions (Q.md)

This file contains 10 practice questions on Statistics Problems for the SAT. Each question includes a multiple-choice layout followed by hints and guided steps — **no answers are given**. Work through the hints to find the solution yourself, Student!

### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

The test scores of 5 students are: 85, 90, 80, 95, and 80. What is the mode of these test scores?

A) 80

B) 85

C) 86

D) 90

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the definition of the mode: the mode is the value that appears most frequently in a data set.
2. Count how many times each score appears in the list:
   - 80 appears twice.
   - 85 appears once.
   - 90 appears once.
   - 95 appears once.
3. Identify the score with the highest frequency.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

What is the median of the following set of numbers?
3, 11, 7, 5, 14, 9, 12

A) 5

B) 7

C) 9

D) 11

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall that to find the median, you must first arrange the numbers in order from least to greatest.
2. Sort the given list: `3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 14`.
3. Count the number of values in the list (there are 7 values, which is an odd number).
4. Find the middle number in the sorted list. Since there are 7 values, the middle number is the 4th value.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

The average (arithmetic mean) of four numbers is 15. If three of the numbers are 12, 18, and 14, what is the fourth number?

A) 14

B) 15

C) 16

D) 20

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the mean formula: `Mean = Sum of all values / Number of values`.
2. Set up the equation using the given values: `(12 + 18 + 14 + x) / 4 = 15`.
3. Multiply both sides by 4 to find the total sum of the four numbers: `Sum = 15 * 4 = 60`.
4. Add the three known numbers together: `12 + 18 + 14`.
5. Subtract this sum from the total sum (60) to find the fourth number `x`.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

A set of 6 numbers has an average (arithmetic mean) of 20. If one number is removed from the set, the average of the remaining 5 numbers is 18. What was the value of the removed number?

A) 18

B) 20

C) 25

D) 30

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the total sum of the original 6 numbers by multiplying their count by their average: `6 * 20 = 120`.
2. Find the total sum of the remaining 5 numbers after one is removed by multiplying their count by their new average: `5 * 18 = 90`.
3. Calculate the value of the removed number by finding the difference between the original sum and the new sum: `Original Sum - New Sum = 120 - 90`.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

A dot plot shows the distribution of the hours studied by a group of students. The hours studied are: one student studied 1 hour, three students studied 2 hours, four students studied 3 hours, two students studied 5 hours, and one student studied 6 hours. What is the range of hours studied?

A) 3

B) 4

C) 5

D) 6

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the definition of the range: the range is the difference between the maximum value and the minimum value in a data set.
2. Identify the minimum number of hours studied in the set (which is 1 hour).
3. Identify the maximum number of hours studied in the set (which is 6 hours).
4. Subtract the minimum value from the maximum value: `6 - 1`.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

Two classes, Class A and Class B, take the same math exam. Both classes have a mean score of 80. The standard deviation of the scores for Class A is 5, and the standard deviation of the scores for Class B is 10. Which of the following statements must be true?

A) The scores in Class A have more variability than the scores in Class B.

B) The scores in Class B have more variability than the scores in Class A.

C) The highest score on the exam was in Class B.

D) The lowest score on the exam was in Class A.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the meaning of standard deviation: standard deviation measures the spread or variability of a data set. A larger standard deviation means the data values are more spread out from the mean.
2. Compare the standard deviations of both classes: Class A has 5, and Class B has 10.
3. Determine which class has a larger spread of scores (more variability).
4. Match this conclusion to the multiple-choice options. (Note: standard deviation does not tell you the exact minimum or maximum scores, so discard C and D).

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium

A set of numbers consists of: 5, 8, 12, 15, and x. If the median of the set is 12, which of the following could be the value of x?

A) 6

B) 9

C) 10

D) 13

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Sort the known values: `5, 8, 12, 15`. There are 4 known values.
2. The total number of values including `x` is 5 (which is an odd number). The median of 5 values is the middle number (the 3rd number when sorted).
3. We are given that the median is 12. This means that when the five numbers are sorted, 12 must be in the 3rd position.
4. For 12 to be in the 3rd position:
   - There must be two numbers less than or equal to 12.
   - There must be two numbers greater than or equal to 12.
5. Look at the known numbers: 5 and 8 are less than 12 (two numbers), and 15 is greater than 12 (one number).
6. To keep 12 in the 3rd position, the variable `x` must be positioned at or to the right of 12 (meaning `x` must be greater than or equal to 12).
7. Review the options to find which number is greater than or equal to 12.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Hard

A box plot summarizes a data set. The median is 50, the first quartile (Q1) is 40, the third quartile (Q3) is 70, the minimum value is 20, and the maximum value is 90. What is the interquartile range (IQR) of this data set?

A) 20

B) 30

C) 50

D) 70

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the formula for the interquartile range: `IQR = Q3 - Q1`.
2. Identify the value of the third quartile `Q3` (which is 70).
3. Identify the value of the first quartile `Q1` (which is 40).
4. Subtract `Q1` from `Q3`: `70 - 40`.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

The average (arithmetic mean) age of a group of 10 people is 25 years. When a new person joins the group, the average age of the 11 people becomes 26 years. What is the age, in years, of the new person?

A) 26

B) 30

C) 36

D) 40

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Calculate the total combined age of the original 10 people: `10 * 25 = 250` years.
2. Calculate the total combined age of the 11 people after the new person joins: `11 * 26`.
3. Multiply: `11 * 26 = 286`.
4. Find the age of the new person by subtracting the original combined age from the new combined age: `286 - 250`.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

The weights, in pounds, of 8 packages are: 12, 15, 18, 12, 22, 25, 15, and 12. If a package weighing 30 pounds is added to the data set, how will this affect the median?

A) The median will increase by 1.5.

B) The median will increase by 3.

C) The median will decrease by 1.

D) The median will remain unchanged.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the median of the original 8 packages first:
   - Sort the values: `12, 12, 12, 15, 15, 18, 22, 25`.
   - Since there are 8 values (an even number), the median is the average of the two middle numbers (the 4th and 5th numbers): `(15 + 15) / 2 = 15`.
2. Find the median of the new set of 9 packages after adding the 30-pound package:
   - Sort the new list: `12, 12, 12, 15, 15, 18, 22, 25, 30`.
   - Since there are 9 values (an odd number), the median is the middle number (the 5th number). Identify this 5th number.
3. Compare the original median to the new median to determine the change.
