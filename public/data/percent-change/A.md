### Question 1
**Correct Answer: C ($68)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Identify the original price and the discount percentage: Original Price = $80, Discount = 15%.
2. Calculate the discount amount: 15% of $80 = 0.15 * 80 = $12.
3. Subtract the discount from the original price to find the sale price: $80 - $12 = $68.
*Alternative method:* A 15% discount means paying 85% of the original price: 0.85 * 80 = $68.

### Question 2
**Correct Answer: C (25%)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Identify the initial and final values: Initial enrollment = 40 students, Final enrollment = 50 students.
2. Find the amount of increase: 50 - 40 = 10 students.
3. Divide the increase by the initial value: 10 / 40 = 0.25.
4. Convert the decimal to a percentage: 0.25 * 100 = 25%.

### Question 5
**Correct Answer: B (The value of the antique increases by 6% each year.)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Identify the parts of the exponential growth function V(t) = P * (1 + r)^t, where P is the initial amount and r is the growth rate as a decimal.
2. In the equation V(t) = 350 * (1.06)^t, the base is 1.06.
3. Set 1 + r = 1.06, which gives r = 0.06.
4. Convert the decimal growth rate to a percentage: 0.06 * 100 = 6% increase per year.

### Question 6
**Correct Answer: B ($198)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**

2. Find the value after the second year's 10% decrease: The decrease is applied to the first-year value ($220), not the original $200.
3. Calculate the final value: $220 * (1 - 0.10) = $220 * 0.90 = $198.

### Question 7
**Correct Answer: B (The population of the city increases by 8% every 5 years.)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Look at the base of the exponential term: 1.08 represents a growth rate of 8% (since 1.08 = 1 + 0.08).
2. Look at the exponent: t/5. This means the 8% growth occurs when the exponent increases by 1.
3. The exponent t/5 increases by 1 every time t increases by 5 (since 5/5 = 1, 10/5 = 2, etc.).
4. Therefore, the 8% increase occurs once every 5 years.

### Question 9
**Correct Answer: A (20%)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Let the original price be P. After a 25% increase, the new price is 1.25 * P.
2. Let d be the decimal percent decrease needed to return from 1.25 * P to P.
3. Set up the equation: (1.25 * P) * (1 - d) = P.
4. Divide both sides by P: 1.25 * (1 - d) = 1.
5. Solve for 1 - d: 1 - d = 1 / 1.25 = 0.80.
6. Solve for d: d = 1 - 0.80 = 0.20 (which is 20%).

### Question 11
**Correct Answer: B ($20.00)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Let the cost of production be C.
2. The manufacturer sells to the wholesaler at a 20% profit: Price = 1.20 * C.
3. The wholesaler sells to the retailer at a 25% markup: Price = (1.20 * C) * 1.25 = 1.50 * C.
4. The retailer sells to the customer at a 30% markup: Price = (1.50 * C) * 1.30 = 1.95 * C.
5. Set the final price equal to the customer's purchase price: 1.95 * C = 39.00.
6. Solve for C: C = 39.00 / 1.95 = 20.00.

### Question 12
**Correct Answer: B (10%)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Start with the given formula: M(t) = M0 * (0.81)^(t/2).
2. Rewrite the expression to find the annual growth/decay factor by grouping the 1/2 with the base: M(t) = M0 * ((0.81)^(1/2))^t.
3. Simplify the base: (0.81)^(1/2) is the square root of 0.81, which is 0.90.
4. The rewritten formula is: M(t) = M0 * (0.90)^t.
5. A base of 0.90 represents 1 - r, where r is the annual decay rate: 1 - r = 0.90, which means r = 0.10.
6. Convert the decimal rate to a percentage: 0.10 * 100 = 10% decrease each year.

### Question 13
**Correct Answer: B (It increases by 8%.)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Let the original radius be r and height be h. The original volume of the cylinder is V = pi * r^2 * h.
2. The new radius is 1.20 * r and the new height is 0.75 * h.
3. Calculate the new volume: V_new = pi * (1.20 * r)^2 * (0.75 * h).
4. Expand the squared term: (1.20 * r)^2 = 1.44 * r^2.
5. Substitute back and multiply the coefficients: V_new = pi * 1.44 * r^2 * 0.75 * h = (1.44 * 0.75) * pi * r^2 * h.
6. Calculate 1.44 * 0.75 = 1.08.
7. V_new = 1.08 * V, which represents an 8% increase.

### Question 14
**Correct Answer: A (5)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Let x be the volume of Solution A used and y be the volume of Solution B used.
2. Set up the total volume equation: x + y = 20.
3. Set up the acid content equation: 0.10 * x + 0.30 * y = 0.15 * 20.
4. Simplify the acid equation: 0.10 * x + 0.30 * y = 3.
5. Express x in terms of y from the first equation: x = 20 - y.
6. Substitute x into the acid equation: 0.10 * (20 - y) + 0.30 * y = 3.
7. Expand and solve for y:
   2 - 0.10 * y + 0.30 * y = 3
   2 + 0.20 * y = 3
   0.20 * y = 1
   y = 1 / 0.20 = 5.
8. Therefore, 5 liters of Solution B were used.

### Question 15
**Correct Answer: B (20%)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1. Let P be the population in 2000 and L be the land area in 2000.
2. The land area per person in 2000 is L / P.
3. In 2010, the population is 1.10 * P (10% increase) and the land area is 0.88 * L (12% decrease).
4. The land area per person in 2010 is (0.88 * L) / (1.10 * P) = (0.88 / 1.10) * (L / P).
5. Simplify the fraction: 0.88 / 1.10 = 8.8 / 11 = 0.80.
6. The land area per person in 2010 is 0.80 times the land area per person in 2000.
7. A factor of 0.80 represents a 20% decrease (1 - 0.80 = 0.20, or 20%).
