### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

A bag contains red marbles and blue marbles. The ratio of red marbles to blue marbles is 3:5. If there are 40 marbles in total, how many red marbles are in the bag?

A) 15

B) 24

C) 25

D) 30

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Identify the ratio of red marbles to blue marbles, which is 3:5. This means for every 3 red marbles, there are 5 blue marbles.
2. Find the total number of parts in the ratio by adding the parts together (3 + 5).
3. Set up a fraction representing the portion of red marbles to the total number of marbles: red parts / total parts.
4. Multiply this fraction by the total number of marbles (40) to find the number of red marbles.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

If 4 notebooks cost $6.00, how much would 10 of the same notebooks cost?

A) $10.00

B) $12.00

C) $15.00

D) $24.00

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Set up a proportion comparing the number of notebooks to their cost: (number of notebooks) / (cost of notebooks) = (new number of notebooks) / (new cost).
2. Write this as: 4 / 6 = 10 / x, where x is the cost of 10 notebooks.
3. Solve for x by cross-multiplying (4 times x = 6 times 10) and then dividing.
4. Alternatively, find the unit price of one notebook first by dividing the total cost ($6.00) by the number of notebooks (4), and then multiply that unit price by 10.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

On a map, 1.5 inches represents 60 miles. If two cities are 4 inches apart on the map, what is the actual distance, in miles, between the two cities?

A) 90

B) 160

C) 180

D) 240

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Establish the ratio of map distance to actual distance: 1.5 inches / 60 miles.
2. Set this ratio equal to the ratio for the two cities: 1.5 / 60 = 4 / d, where d is the actual distance in miles.
3. Cross-multiply to solve for d: 1.5 times d = 60 times 4.
4. Divide both sides by 1.5 to isolate d and find the actual distance.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

In a bakery, the ratio of the number of chocolate cookies to the number of vanilla cookies is 2:3, and the ratio of the number of vanilla cookies to the number of strawberry cookies is 4:5. What is the ratio of chocolate cookies to strawberry cookies?

A) 2:5

B) 8:15

C) 6:15

D) 1:2

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Write down the two ratios: chocolate to vanilla (C:V = 2:3) and vanilla to strawberry (V:S = 4:5).
2. To compare chocolate (C) and strawberry (S) directly, find a common term for the shared item, which is vanilla (V). The V values in the two ratios are 3 and 4.
3. Find the least common multiple (LCM) of 3 and 4.
4. Multiply the terms of the first ratio (C:V) so that the V term equals that LCM.
5. Multiply the terms of the second ratio (V:S) so that the V term also equals that LCM.
6. Now that the vanilla term is the same in both ratios, compare the new chocolate term directly to the new strawberry term to find the ratio C:S.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

The time, t, in hours, required to paint a fence is inversely proportional to the number of workers, w, working at a constant rate. If it takes 3 workers 6 hours to paint the fence, how many hours would it take 4 workers to paint the same fence?

A) 4.5

B) 5.0

C) 8.0

D) 9.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the definition of an inverse proportion: the product of the two variables is constant. That is, w times t = k, where k is a constant representing the total worker-hours.
2. Use the given values (3 workers and 6 hours) to calculate the constant k.
3. Set up the equation for the new situation with 4 workers: 4 times t = k.
4. Solve for t by dividing the constant k by 4.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

In a certain class, the ratio of students who play a musical instrument to students who do not is 3:7. If there are 12 more students who do not play an instrument than students who do, how many students are in the class in total?

A) 21

B) 30

C) 42

D) 70

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let the number of students who play an instrument be 3x and the number of students who do not be 7x, where x is a common multiplier.
2. Write an equation representing the difference between the two groups: 7x - 3x = 12.
3. Solve for x.
4. The total number of students in the class is the sum of both groups: 3x + 7x = 10x. Multiply your value of x by 10 to get the total.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

A chemist has 8 liters of a solution that is 20% acid by volume. How many liters of pure acid must be added to this solution to create a new solution that is 50% acid?

A) 2.4

B) 3.6

C) 4.8

D) 6.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Calculate the initial volume of pure acid in the 8-liter solution: 20% of 8 liters (0.20 times 8).
2. Let x be the number of liters of pure acid added.
3. Write an expression for the total volume of pure acid in the new solution: (initial acid volume + x).
4. Write an expression for the total volume of the new solution: (8 + x).
5. Set up an equation representing that the volume of pure acid is 50% (or 0.50) of the total volume: (initial acid volume + x) / (8 + x) = 0.50.
6. Solve the equation for x.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

A printer prints pages at a constant rate. If it can print 45 pages in 3 minutes, how many pages can it print in x minutes?

A) 15x

B) 45x

C) x / 15

D) 3x / 45

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Determine the printing rate per minute (pages per minute) by dividing the number of pages by the number of minutes: 45 / 3.
2. This value represents the unit rate (how many pages are printed in 1 minute).
3. Since the printing rate is constant, multiply this unit rate by x minutes to find the expression for the total pages printed in x minutes.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

A box contains red, blue, and green pens in the ratio 2:3:5. If 4 blue pens are removed and 6 green pens are added, the new ratio of red pens to green pens becomes 1:3. What was the initial number of blue pens in the box?

A) 12

B) 18

C) 20

D) 24

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let the initial number of red, blue, and green pens be 2x, 3x, and 5x, respectively.
2. Express the new quantities after the changes: red pens = 2x (unchanged), blue pens = 3x - 4, green pens = 5x + 6.
3. Set up the proportion for the new ratio of red pens to green pens, which is 1:3: (new red) / (new green) = 1 / 3.
4. Substitute the expressions: 2x / (5x + 6) = 1 / 3.
5. Cross-multiply to solve for x: 3 times 2x = 1 times (5x + 6).
6. Solve for x.
7. Use this value of x to calculate the initial number of blue pens, which is 3x.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

The intensity of light from a source is inversely proportional to the square of the distance from the source. If the intensity is 16 units at a distance of 3 meters, what is the intensity of the light at a distance of 6 meters?

A) 4

B) 8

C) 32

D) 64

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall that the relationship of inverse proportion to the square of distance can be written as: Intensity times (Distance squared) = k, where k is a constant.
2. Use the initial values (Intensity = 16, Distance = 3) to find the value of k: 16 times (3 squared) = k.
3. Use this constant k to find the new intensity, I, at a distance of 6 meters: I times (6 squared) = k.
4. Solve for I.
5. Alternatively, observe how the distance changed: it doubled from 3 to 6. Since intensity is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, doubling the distance will divide the intensity by 2 squared (which is 4). Divide the original intensity by this factor.
