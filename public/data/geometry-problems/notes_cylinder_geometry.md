# SAT Math Study Notes: Cylinder Geometry & Volume

Mastering geometry formulas and word problem calculations is critical for the final questions of the Math modules.

---

## 1. Right Circular Cylinders
A cylinder is a 3D solid with two congruent circular bases and a curved side.

### Key Formulas
*   **Volume (V):**
    V = pi * r^2 * h
    *   **r:** Radius of the circular base.
    *   **h:** Height of the cylinder.
*   **Area of circular base (A):**
    A = pi * r^2
*   **Circumference of circular base (C):**
    C = 2 * pi * r

---

## 2. Common Cylinder Traps on the SAT

### Trap 1: Diameter vs. Radius
*   **The Trap:** The question will give you the *diameter* of the cylinder's base, but the formula requires the *radius*.
*   **The Fix:** Always write down:
    radius = diameter / 2
    Do this immediately before plugging numbers into the volume formula. 
    *Example:* If the base diameter is 22, the radius is 11. (Plugin r = 11, not 22!).

### Trap 2: Leaving Answers in Terms of Pi
*   **The Trap:** The SAT choices or grid-in directions might ask for the answer in terms of pi (e.g., 726*pi) rather than decimal format (e.g., 2280.8).
*   **The Fix:** Don't multiply by 3.14159 unless the question asks for a rounded decimal. Keep "pi" as a variable symbol in your scratch work.

### Trap 3: Units Conversion
*   **The Trap:** Giving dimensions in inches but asking for volume in feet, or giving height in centimeters and radius in millimeters.
*   **The Fix:** Convert all dimensions to the target unit *before* you calculate the volume.

## 📝 Practice Plan for Student
1.  Draw the cylinder on your scratch paper.
2.  Label the height (h) and radius (r).
3.  Write out the formula `V = pi * r^2 * h` in plain text.
4.  Solve step-by-step.
