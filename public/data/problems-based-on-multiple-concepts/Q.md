### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

The length of a rectangle is 3 times its width. If the perimeter of the rectangle is 40, what is the area of the rectangle?

A) 15

B) 30

C) 75

D) 300

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let the width of the rectangle be `w`. Since the length is 3 times the width, let the length be `3w`.
2. Recall the perimeter formula: `Perimeter = 2 * (length + width)`.
3. Set up the equation using the given perimeter: `2 * (3w + w) = 40`.
4. Simplify: `2 * 4w = 40` -> `8w = 40`. Solve for the width `w`.
5. Calculate the length: `3 * w`.
6. Recall the area formula: `Area = length * width`. Substitute your calculated width and length to find the area.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

In right triangle ABC, the lengths of the legs are 6 and 8. What is the value of sin(A) + cos(A), where the leg of length 6 is opposite to angle A?

A) 1

B) 7/5

C) 8/5

D) 12/5

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the length of the hypotenuse `c` using the Pythagorean theorem: `6^2 + 8^2 = c^2` -> `36 + 64 = 100`. Take the square root to find the hypotenuse.
2. Recall the trigonometric ratio definitions:
   - `sin(A) = Opposite / Hypotenuse`
   - `cos(A) = Adjacent / Hypotenuse`
3. Identify the sides relative to angle A: the opposite side is 6, and the adjacent side is the other leg (which is 8).
4. Calculate `sin(A)` and `cos(A)` as fractions.
5. Add the two fractions together.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

The average (arithmetic mean) of three numbers is 10. If the ratio of the first two numbers is 1:2 and the third number is 15, what is the first number?

A) 5

B) 10

C) 15

D) 20

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let the first number be `x` and the second number be `2x` based on their 1:2 ratio. The third number is given as 15.
2. Recall the average formula: `(Number1 + Number2 + Number3) / 3 = Average`.
3. Set up the equation: `(x + 2x + 15) / 3 = 10`.
4. Multiply both sides by 3: `3x + 15 = 30`.
5. Solve for `x` (this represents the first number).

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

A circle with a radius of 5 is inscribed inside a square so that the circle touches all four sides of the square. What is the area of the region inside the square but outside the circle?

A) 25 - 5\pi

B) 100 - 5\pi

C) 100 - 25\pi

D) 100 - 100\pi

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the side length of the square: since the circle is inscribed inside the square, the side length of the square is equal to the diameter of the circle. The diameter is `2 * radius = 2 * 5 = 10`.
2. Find the area of the square: `Side^2 = 10^2 = 100`.
3. Find the area of the circle: `\pi * r^2 = \pi * 5^2 = 25\pi`.
4. Calculate the area of the region outside the circle by subtracting the area of the circle from the area of the square.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

If f(x) = 2x + 3 and g(x) = x^2 - 4, what is the value of f(g(3))?

A) 5

B) 9

C) 13

D) 21

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. This is a composite function problem. Evaluate the inner function first: `g(3)`.
2. Substitute 3 into the equation for `g(x)`: `g(3) = 3^2 - 4`. Simplify the calculation.
3. Take the output value from Step 2 and substitute it as the input `x` in the outer function `f(x)`.
4. Evaluate `f(output)` to find the final result.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

In the xy-plane, a line with a slope of 2 intersects the y-axis at (0, 3). If this line also intersects the parabola y = x^2 at two points, what are the x-coordinates of these points of intersection?

A) -3 and 1

B) -1 and 3

C) -3 and -1

D) 1 and 3

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Set up the equation of the line using the slope `m = 2` and y-intercept `b = 3`: `y = 2x + 3`.
2. To find the points of intersection with the parabola `y = x^2`, set the line equation equal to the parabola equation: `x^2 = 2x + 3`.
3. Move all terms to one side to form a standard quadratic equation: `x^2 - 2x - 3 = 0`.
4. Factor the quadratic equation: find two numbers that multiply to -3 and add to -2.
5. Solve for the two x-coordinate values.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium

The prices of two items are in the ratio 3:5. If the price of the first item is increased by 20% and the price of the second item is decreased by 10%, what is the new ratio of their prices?

A) 4:5

B) 6:5

C) 12:25

D) 18:25

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let the original prices of the two items be `3x` and `5x` respectively.
2. Apply the percentage changes to the prices:
   - First item price after 20% increase: `3x * 1.20 = 3.6x`.
   - Second item price after 10% decrease: `5x * 0.90 = 4.5x`.
3. Set up the new ratio using these values: `3.6x : 4.5x`.
4. Simplify the ratio. Cancel the `x` term and multiply both numbers by 10 to clear the decimals: `36 : 45`.
5. Reduce this ratio by dividing both numbers by their greatest common divisor (which is 9).

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Hard

A right circular cylinder has a height of 10 and a volume of 90\pi. What is the total surface area of the cylinder?

A) 18\pi

B) 60\pi

C) 78\pi

D) 118\pi

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the radius `r` of the cylinder first using the volume formula: `Volume = \pi * r^2 * h`.
2. Substitute the given values: `90\pi = \pi * r^2 * 10`.
3. Divide both sides by `10\pi`: `9 = r^2`. Take the square root to find the radius `r`.
4. Recall the formula for the total surface area of a cylinder: `Surface Area = 2\pi * r^2 + 2\pi * r * h`.
5. Substitute the radius `r = 3` and height `h = 10` into the surface area formula.
6. Calculate the values and combine the terms.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

A bag contains 4 red balls and 6 blue balls. A ball is drawn at random and its color is recorded. If it is red, it is returned to the bag along with 2 additional blue balls. If it is blue, it is returned to the bag along with 2 additional red balls. What is the probability that the second ball drawn at random from the bag is red?

A) 12/55

B) 24/55

C) 36/55

D) 48/55

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Use the law of total probability. There are two independent paths to draw a red ball on the second turn:
   - **Path 1:** Red first, then Red second.
   - **Path 2:** Blue first, then Red second.
2. Calculate the probability of Path 1:
   - P(Red first) = `4 / 10`.
   - The red ball is returned, and 2 blue balls are added. Composition of bag now: `4 red` and `8 blue` (total 12 balls).
   - P(Red second given Red first) = `4 / 12` (which is `1 / 3`).
   - Multiply the two probabilities: `(4 / 10) * (1 / 3) = 4 / 30 = 2 / 15`.
3. Calculate the probability of Path 2:
   - P(Blue first) = `6 / 10`.
   - The blue ball is returned, and 2 red balls are added. Composition of bag now: `6 red` and `6 blue` (total 12 balls).
   - P(Red second given Blue first) = `6 / 12` (which is `1 / 2`).
   - Multiply the two probabilities: `(6 / 10) * (1 / 2) = 6 / 20 = 3 / 10`.
4. Add the probabilities of the two paths together: `(2 / 15) + (3 / 10)`. Find a common denominator (which is 30) to calculate the sum.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

The sum of the first n terms of a sequence is given by the formula Sn = 2n^2 + 3n. What is the value of the 5th term in this sequence?

A) 21

B) 44

C) 65

D) 109

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the relationship between the terms of a sequence and their partial sums: the n-th term `an` can be calculated as `an = Sn - S(n - 1)`.
2. To find the 5th term `a5`, use the formula: `a5 = S5 - S4`.
3. Calculate `S5` by substituting `n = 5` into the given formula: `2(5)^2 + 3(5) = 2(25) + 15`.
4. Calculate `S4` by substituting `n = 4` into the given formula: `2(4)^2 + 3(4) = 2(16) + 12`.
5. Subtract the value of `S4` from the value of `S5` to find the 5th term.
