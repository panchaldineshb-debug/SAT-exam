# SAT Prep: Multi-Step Variable Problems - Practice Questions (Q.md)

This file contains 10 practice questions on Multi-Step Variable Problems for the SAT. Each question includes a multiple-choice layout followed by hints and guided steps — **no answers are given**. Work through the hints to find the solution yourself, Student!

---

### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

If `3x + 7 = 22`, what is the value of `6x + 5`?

A) 15

B) 25

C) 35

D) 45

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Start by isolating `x` in the equation `3x + 7 = 22`. What operation do you need to do to both sides first?
2. Once you have the value of `x`, do **not** just plug it into `6x + 5` right away — instead, notice a shortcut: how is `6x + 5` related to `3x + 7`?
3. Think about whether you can multiply or adjust the original equation to get `6x` directly, then handle the constant separately. This saves time on the SAT!

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

A store sells notebooks for `$n` each. Maya buys 4 notebooks and a pen that costs `$2` more than a notebook. She pays with a `$50` bill and receives `$13` in change. What is the cost, in dollars, of one notebook?

A) $5

B) $6

C) $7

D) $8

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. First, figure out how much Maya spent in total. If she paid with `$50` and received `$13` in change, what is the total amount she spent?
2. Express the cost of the pen in terms of `n` (the cost of one notebook).
3. Write an equation: the total cost of 4 notebooks plus the pen equals the amount she spent. Set up the equation and collect like terms.
4. Solve for `n` by isolating the variable.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

If `5(2y − 3) = 25`, what is the value of `4y`?

A) 4

B) 8

C) 16

D) 20

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Distribute the `5` on the left side of the equation across the parentheses.
2. Solve the resulting equation for `y` by isolating the variable step by step.
3. Once you have `y`, compute `4y`. Be careful — the question asks for `4y`, not just `y`!

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

The sum of three consecutive even integers is `78`. What is the largest of the three integers?

A) 24

B) 26

C) 28

D) 30

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let the smallest of the three consecutive even integers be `x`. How would you express the next two consecutive even integers in terms of `x`? (Remember: consecutive **even** integers differ by 2, not 1.)
2. Write an equation: the three integers sum to `78`. Combine like terms and solve for `x`.
3. Once you have `x` (the smallest), use it to find the **largest** of the three integers. Be sure you answer for the largest, not the smallest!

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

A school fundraiser sells small candles for `$4` each and large candles for `$7` each. A total of 60 candles were sold, and the fundraiser collected `$318`. How many large candles were sold?

A) 18

B) 26

C) 36

D) 42

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. This is a system of two equations with two unknowns. Let `s` = number of small candles sold and `l` = number of large candles sold.
2. Write your **first equation** using the total number of candles: `s + l = 60`.
3. Write your **second equation** using the total money collected: `4s + 7l = 318`.
4. Use substitution: solve the first equation for `s` in terms of `l`, then substitute into the second equation.
5. Solve for `l`. Check your answer by verifying that both equations are satisfied.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

If `(ax + 3)(bx - 5) = 12x^2 - cx - 15` for all values of `x`, and `a + b = 7`, what is the value of `c`?

A) 1

B) 7

C) 9

D) 11

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Expand the left side `(ax + 3)(bx - 5)` by using the FOIL method (First, Outer, Inner, Last).
2. After expanding, identify the coefficient of `x^2` on the left side. Since the coefficient of `x^2` on the right side is `12`, set up the equation `ab = 12`.
3. You now have two equations: `a + b = 7` and `ab = 12`. Think about what values of `a` and `b` satisfy both. (Hint: think of two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7.)
4. Identify the coefficient of `x` after expanding the left side. This will be expressed in terms of `a` and `b`. Set it equal to `-c` from the right side, and solve for `c`.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

A car rental company charges a flat fee of `$25` plus `$0.40` per mile driven. A second company charges `$10` plus `$0.65` per mile driven. For what number of miles driven would the total cost be the same for both companies?

A) 40

B) 50

C) 60

D) 70

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Write a cost expression for Company 1 in terms of miles driven, `m`: Cost1 = 25 + 0.40m.
2. Write a cost expression for Company 2: Cost2 = 10 + 0.65m.
3. Set the two cost expressions equal to each other, since you want to find the mileage where both companies charge the same amount.
4. Solve the resulting equation for `m`. You may find it easier to multiply through by 100 first to eliminate the decimals.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

If `x` and `y` are positive numbers satisfying the equations below, what is the value of `x - y`?

    3x + 2y = 41
    x - 2y = 3

A) -6

B) 6

C) 7

D) 11

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Look at the two equations side by side. Notice that one equation contains `+2y` and the other contains `-2y`. What happens if you **add** both equations directly? The `y` terms will cancel!
2. After adding the equations, you will have a single equation with only `x`. Solve for `x`.
3. Substitute the value of `x` back into either original equation to find `y`.
4. Finally, compute `x - y`. Double-check by plugging both values into the **other** original equation to verify.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

The function `f` is defined by `f(x) = 2x^2 - kx + 3`. If `f(3) = 12`, what is the value of `f(-2)`?

A) 3

B) 13

C) 17

D) 21

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Step 1 — Find k: Substitute `x = 3` into `f(x) = 2x^2 - kx + 3` and set the result equal to `12`. Solve this equation for `k`.
2. Step 2 — Rewrite the function: Once you know `k`, write out the complete function with the actual numerical value in place of `k`.
3. Step 3 — Evaluate at x = -2: Substitute `x = -2` into your updated function. Be careful with signs when squaring a negative number and when multiplying by `k`.
4. Simplify the expression step by step to get your final answer.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

In the system of equations below, `p` and `q` are constants. If the system has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of `p + q`?

    4x - 6y = 10
    px + qy = 25

A) -5

B) -1

C) 5

D) 7

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the condition for a system of two linear equations to have **infinitely many solutions**: the two equations must represent the **same line**, meaning one equation is a scalar multiple of the other.
2. Look at the constants on the right-hand side: the second equation equals `25` and the first equals `10`. What number do you multiply `10` by to get `25`? This is your scalar multiplier.
3. Apply that same scalar multiplier to **both coefficients** on the left side of the first equation to find the values of `p` and `q`.
4. Once you have `p` and `q`, compute `p + q`.
