# 📊 Scoring Rubric: SAT Practice Test Suite (August 20, 2026)

This test is a **Half-Length Gauntlet Simulation**. It is out of 30 total raw points (15 R&W, 15 Math).

To estimate your scaled score (out of 1600):

## 1. Calculate Your Raw Scores
*   **Reading & Writing Raw Score:** Number of correct answers in Section 1 (Out of 15).
*   **Math Raw Score:** Number of correct answers in Section 2 (Out of 15).
