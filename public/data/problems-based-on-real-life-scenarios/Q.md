### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

A taxi driver charges a flat fee of $3.50 plus $2.00 per mile for a ride. Which of the following functions represents the cost C(m), in dollars, for a ride of m miles?

A) C(m) = 3.50m + 2.00

B) C(m) = 2.00m + 3.50

C) C(m) = 5.50m

D) C(m) = 2.00 + 3.50 / m

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Identify the constant fee (the y-intercept): this is the flat fee of $3.50 charged regardless of the distance.
2. Identify the variable rate (the slope): this is the cost per mile of $2.00.
3. Combine these into a linear equation of the form `y = mx + b`, where `m` is the rate per mile and `b` is the flat fee.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

A population of 200 birds in a wildlife reserve increases at a constant rate of 5% each year. Which of the following functions represents the population P(t) of the birds after t years?

A) P(t) = 200 * (0.05)^t

B) P(t) = 200 * (1.05)^t

C) P(t) = 200 + 1.05t

D) P(t) = 200 + 5t

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Identify the type of growth: since the population increases by a percentage each year, this represents exponential growth. The formula is `P(t) = P0 * (1 + r)^t`.
2. Identify the initial population `P0` (which is 200).
3. Identify the growth rate `r` as a decimal: `5% = 0.05`.
4. Calculate the growth factor: `1 + r = 1 + 0.05 = 1.05`.
5. Assemble the function with these values.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

A shipping warehouse starts the day with 500 boxes. If 15 boxes are shipped out every hour during a shift, which of the following functions represents the number of boxes remaining, B(d), after d hours?

A) B(d) = 500 + 15d

B) B(d) = 500 - 15d

C) B(d) = 15d - 500

D) B(d) = 500 * (15)^d

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Identify the starting number of boxes: this is the initial constant value (500).
2. Determine if the count is increasing or decreasing: since boxes are being shipped *out*, the total count decreases over time.
3. Identify the rate of change: 15 boxes per hour.
4. Set up a linear equation representing the subtraction of the hourly shipped boxes from the initial amount: `Remaining = Initial - Rate * Time`.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

A cell phone plan charges a flat fee of $40 per month plus $0.10 per text message sent. If a customer's total bill for one month was $55, how many text messages did the customer send?

A) 15

B) 55

C) 150

D) 400

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Set up an equation representing the monthly cost: `Flat Fee + Rate * Messages = Total Bill`.
2. Substitute the given values: `40 + 0.10 * x = 55`, where `x` is the number of text messages.
3. Isolate the variable term `0.10x` by subtracting 40 from both sides: `0.10x = 15`.
4. Solve for `x` by dividing both sides of the equation by 0.10. (Hint: dividing by 0.10 is the same as multiplying by 10).

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

A savings account starts with $1,000 and earns 4% interest compounded annually. Which of the following expressions represents the total value in the account after 5 years?

A) 1,000 * (1.04)^5

B) 1,000 + 1,000 * 0.04 * 5

C) 1,000 * (0.04)^5

D) 1,000 * (1.4)^5

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the compound interest formula: `A = P * (1 + r)^t`, where `P` is the principal, `r` is the annual interest rate as a decimal, and `t` is the number of years.
2. Identify the values from the prompt:
   - Principal `P = 1,000`
   - Rate `r = 4% = 0.04`
   - Time `t = 5`
3. Substitute these values into the formula: `1,000 * (1 + 0.04)^5`.
4. Simplify the base inside the parentheses.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

A company sells custom t-shirts for $15 each. The total cost, C(x), in dollars, to produce x t-shirts is given by the function C(x) = 5x + 200. How many t-shirts must the company sell to break even (where total revenue equals total cost)?

A) 10

B) 20

C) 40

D) 50

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Set up the expression for total revenue: since each t-shirt is sold for $15, the revenue for `x` t-shirts is `15x`.
2. Recall that breaking even means: `Total Revenue = Total Cost`.
3. Set up the equation using the cost function: `15x = 5x + 200`.
4. Solve for `x` by subtracting `5x` from both sides, then dividing by the remaining coefficient.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium

The velocity, v, of a car decelerating at a constant rate is modeled by the function v(t) = -5t + 30, where v is the velocity in meters per second and t is the time in seconds since the brakes were applied. What does the number 30 represent in this context?

A) The deceleration rate of the car, in meters per second squared.

B) The initial velocity of the car when the brakes were applied, in meters per second.

C) The time it takes for the car to stop completely, in seconds.

D) The total distance traveled by the car while braking, in meters.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Analyze the linear equation `v(t) = -5t + 30` which is in the form `y = mx + b`.
2. In this function, the constant term `30` is the vertical intercept (y-intercept), which is the value of the function when time `t = 0`.
3. Interpret what `t = 0` means in the context of the scenario (the moment the braking process started).
4. Match this definition to the options.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Hard

A radioactive substance decays at a rate of 12% every 4 hours. If the initial mass of the substance is 80 grams, which of the following functions represents the mass remaining, M(t), in grams, after t hours?

A) M(t) = 80 * (0.88)^(t/4)

B) M(t) = 80 * (0.12)^(t/4)

C) M(t) = 80 * (0.88)^(4t)

D) M(t) = 80 - 0.12 * (t / 4)

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Since the substance decays, the remaining mass is calculated by multiplying the initial mass by the decay factor.
2. If it decays by 12%, the percentage remaining after each period is: `100% - 12% = 88%`, which is `0.88` as a decimal. This is the base of the exponent.
3. The decay happens *every 4 hours*. This means that the number of decay periods in `t` hours is `t / 4`.
4. Assemble the exponential decay function: `M(t) = (Initial Mass) * (Decay Factor)^periods`.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

A professional house painter can paint a room in 4 hours, and his apprentice can paint the same room in 6 hours. If they work together at their constant rates, how many hours will it take them to paint the room?

A) 2.4

B) 4.8

C) 5.0

D) 10.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Determine the individual rate of work per hour for each person:
   - Painter's rate: `1 / 4` of the room per hour.
   - Apprentice's rate: `1 / 6` of the room per hour.
2. Calculate their combined rate of work when working together by adding the two fractions: `1/4 + 1/6`.
3. Find a common denominator (which is 12) to add them: `3/12 + 2/12 = 5/12` of the room per hour.
4. Recall that the time required to complete the task is the reciprocal of the combined rate: `Time = 1 / Combined Rate = 1 / (5/12) = 12 / 5`.
5. Convert the fraction `12 / 5` into a decimal.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

An online subscription service has 10,000 members. Market research shows that for every $1 increase in the monthly price, they will lose 500 members. If the current monthly price is $10, which of the following functions represents the monthly revenue, R(x), in dollars, if they increase the price by x dollars?

A) R(x) = (10 + x)(10,000 - 500x)

B) R(x) = (10 - x)(10,000 + 500x)

C) R(x) = 100,000 - 500x

D) R(x) = (10 + x)(10,000 - 500)

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the revenue formula: `Revenue = Price * Number of members`.
2. Write an expression for the new price after an increase of `x` dollars: `New Price = 10 + x`.
3. Write an expression for the number of members after losing 500 members for every dollar increase: `New Members = 10,000 - 500 * x`.
4. Multiply the two expressions together to find the revenue function `R(x)`.
