# SAT Prep: Advanced Proportion - Practice Questions (Q.md)

This file contains 10 practice questions on Advanced Proportion for the SAT. Each question includes a multiple-choice layout followed by hints and guided steps — **no answers are given**. Work through the hints to find the solution yourself, Student!

---

### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

A machine produces 240 plastic cups every 6 minutes. At this rate, how many cups can the machine produce in 15 minutes?

A) 540

B) 600

C) 660

D) 720

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Determine the production rate per minute. Divide the number of cups (240) by the number of minutes (6).
2. Once you have the rate (cups per minute), multiply that rate by the target time of 15 minutes.
3. Select the option that matches your product.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

On a map, 1.5 inches represents 60 miles. If two cities are 4.5 inches apart on the map, what is the actual distance between them, in miles?

A) 120

B) 180

C) 240

D) 270

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Notice the relationship between 1.5 inches and 4.5 inches. What factor do you need to multiply 1.5 by to get 4.5?
2. Multiply the representative distance (60 miles) by that same scale factor to find the actual distance.
3. Alternatively, set up a proportion: `1.5 / 60 = 4.5 / x` and solve for `x` by cross-multiplying.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

If $a : b = 3 : 4$ and $b : c = 8 : 9$, what is the ratio $a : c$?

A) 1 : 3

B) 2 : 3

C) 3 : 2

D) 4 : 3

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Notice that $b$ is the common link between the two ratios. In the first ratio, the term for $b$ is 4. In the second, it is 8.
2. Multiply the first ratio ($3 : 4$) by 2 so that the value of $b$ matches in both ratios. What does the ratio $a : b$ become?
3. Once the term for $b$ is the same in both ratios, you can directly compare $a$ and $c$ to form the ratio $a : c$. Simplify the ratio if necessary.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

A cyclist travels at a constant speed of 18 kilometers per hour. Which of the following is closest to the cyclist's speed in meters per second? (1 kilometer = 1,000 meters)

A) 5

B) 10

C) 15

D) 20

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Start by converting kilometers to meters: multiply the speed (18 km) by 1,000 to find the speed in meters per hour.
2. Next, convert hours to seconds: recall that 1 hour = 60 minutes and 1 minute = 60 seconds (so 1 hour = 3,600 seconds).
3. Divide your speed in meters per hour by 3,600 to find the speed in meters per second. Simplify the division.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

In a certain school, the ratio of teachers to students is 1 : 15. If there are 240 more students than teachers, what is the total number of teachers and students at the school?

A) 256

B) 260

C) 270

D) 280

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let $x$ represent the number of teachers. If the ratio is 1 : 15, then the number of students is $15x$.
2. The difference between the number of students and teachers is 240. Set up the equation: $15x - x = 240$.
3. Solve for $x$ to find the number of teachers.
4. Add the number of teachers ($x$) and students ($15x$) to get the total population: $16x$. Substitute your value for $x$ and calculate the final count.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

An alloy is made by mixing copper, zinc, and tin in the ratio 5 : 3 : 2 by weight. If a block of this alloy contains 45 grams of copper, what is the total weight, in grams, of the block?

A) 75

B) 90

C) 100

D) 120

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Express the parts of the ratio in terms of a variable $x$. Let the weight of copper be $5x$, zinc be $3x$, and tin be $2x$.
2. You are given that the weight of copper is 45 grams. Set up the equation: $5x = 45$.
3. Solve for $x$.
4. The total weight of the block is the sum of all parts: $5x + 3x + 2x = 10x$. Substitute your value of $x$ into this expression to find the total weight.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

The variable $y$ is inversely proportional to the square of $x$. If $y = 9$ when $x = 2$, what is the value of $y$ when $x = 6$?

A) 1

B) 3

C) 9

D) 18

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the equation for inverse proportionality: $y = k / x^2$, where $k$ is a constant.
2. Substitute the known values ($y = 9$ and $x = 2$) into the equation to solve for the constant $k$.
3. Rewrite the equation with your calculated value of $k$.
4. Substitute $x = 6$ into the equation and solve for $y$. Simplify the fraction.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

A container in the shape of a right circular cylinder is filled with water. The container has a height of 10 centimeters and a base radius of 3 centimeters. If the water is poured into a larger cylindrical container with a base radius of 5 centimeters, what will be the height, in centimeters, of the water in the new container?

A) 3.6

B) 4.5

C) 6.0

D) 7.2

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the formula for the volume of a cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$.
2. Calculate the volume of water in the first container using $r = 3$ and $h = 10$. Keep $\pi$ in your expression (do not convert it to a decimal).
3. The volume of water remains the same when poured into the new container. Set the volume equal to the formula for the new container: $V = \pi (5)^2 h_{new}$.
4. Solve the equation for $h_{new}$ by dividing both sides by $25\pi$.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

If 8 workers can paint a building in 6 days, how many days would it take 12 workers to paint the same building, assuming all workers paint at the same constant rate?

A) 3

B) 4

C) 8

D) 9

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. This is an inverse proportion problem because adding more workers reduces the time required to complete the task.
2. Calculate the total "worker-days" needed to paint the building by multiplying the number of workers (8) by the number of days (6).
3. Set the total worker-days equal to the product of the new number of workers (12) and the unknown number of days ($d$): $12 \times d = \text{total worker-days}$.
4. Solve for $d$.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

A copper wire has a mass of 45 grams and a volume of 5 cubic centimeters. A second copper wire has a mass of 108 grams. If the density of copper is uniform, what is the volume, in cubic centimeters, of the second wire?

A) 9.0

B) 10.8

C) 12.0

D) 15.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Density is the ratio of mass to volume ($D = m / V$). Since the density of copper is uniform, the ratio of mass to volume is constant for both wires.
2. Set up a proportion: $\text{mass}_1 / \text{volume}_1 = \text{mass}_2 / \text{volume}_2$.
3. Substitute the values: $45 / 5 = 108 / V_2$.
4. Simplify the left-hand fraction, then solve for $V_2$.
