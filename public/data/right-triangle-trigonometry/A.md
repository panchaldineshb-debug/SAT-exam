### Question 1
**Correct Answer: B ($\frac{12}{13}$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Identify the sides of the triangle:** In right triangle $ABC$, the right angle is at $C$. The sides forming the right angle are $AC = 5$ (adjacent to angle $A$) and $BC = 12$ (opposite to angle $A$).
2.  **Find the hypotenuse ($AB$):** Use the Pythagorean theorem:
    $$AB^2 = AC^2 + BC^2$$
    $$AB^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 = 25 + 144 = 169$$
    $$AB = \sqrt{169} = 13$$
    *(Note: This is a standard $5-12-13$ Pythagorean triple).*
3.  **Find $\sin(A)$:** Sine is defined as the ratio of the opposite side to the hypotenuse:
    $$\sin(A) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} = \frac{BC}{AB} = \frac{12}{13}$$

### Question 2
**Correct Answer: C ($40$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Recall the Complementary Angle Identity:** For any acute angles $A$ and $B$, if $\sin(A) = \cos(B)$, then the angles are complementary, meaning their sum is $90^\circ$:
    $$A + B = 90^\circ$$
2.  **Set up the equation:** Here, $A = x$ and $B = 2x - 30$.
    $$x + (2x - 30) = 90$$
3.  **Solve for $x$:**
    $$3x - 30 = 90$$
    $$3x = 120$$
    $$x = 40$$

### Question 3
**Correct Answer: A ($\frac{3}{5}$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Recall the Complementary Angle Identity in Radians:** For any acute angle $x$ measured in radians, the sine of the angle is equal to the cosine of its complement:
    $$\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - x\right) = \sin(x)$$
2.  **Substitute the given value:** We are given that $\sin(x) = \frac{3}{5}$. Therefore:
    $$\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - x\right) = \frac{3}{5}$$

### Question 4
**Correct Answer: A ($5$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Identify the triangle type:** Since $\triangle ABC$ is a right triangle with $m\angle B = 90^\circ$ and $m\angle A = 60^\circ$, the third angle must be:
    $$m\angle C = 180^\circ - 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ$$
    This is a $30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ$ special right triangle.
2.  **Use special right triangle ratios:** The ratio of the sides opposite $30^\circ : 60^\circ : 90^\circ$ is $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$, or $x : x\sqrt{3} : 2x$.
    *   The hypotenuse $AC$ is opposite the $90^\circ$ angle, so $\text{Hypotenuse} = 2x = 10$.
    *   This gives $x = 5$.
    *   Side $AB$ is opposite the $30^\circ$ angle ($\angle C$), which corresponds to $x$.
3.  **Calculate $AB$:**
    $$AB = x = 5$$

### Question 5
**Correct Answer: A ($\frac{3}{5}$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Use the definition of tangent:** In right triangle $XYZ$ (with the right angle at $Y$):
    $$\tan(X) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}} = \frac{YZ}{XY} = \frac{3}{4}$$
    We can assign side lengths: opposite leg $YZ = 3$ and adjacent leg $XY = 4$.
2.  **Find the hypotenuse ($XZ$):** Using the Pythagorean theorem:
    $$XZ = \sqrt{XY^2 + YZ^2} = \sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5$$
3.  **Find $\cos(Z)$:** For angle $Z$:
    *   The adjacent side is $YZ = 3$.
    *   The hypotenuse is $XZ = 5$.
    $$\cos(Z) = \frac{\text{Adjacent}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} = \frac{YZ}{XZ} = \frac{3}{5}$$

### Question 6
**Correct Answer: A ($\frac{3}{4}$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Find similar triangles:** Both $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle ADE$ are right triangles ($\angle B = 90^\circ$ and $\angle AED = 90^\circ$) and they share the acute angle $A$. By Angle-Angle (AA) similarity:
    $$\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ACB$$
2.  **Relate the angles:** Since the triangles are similar, their corresponding angles are equal. Specifically, the angle opposite the side $AE$ in $\triangle ADE$ ($\angle ADE$) corresponds to the angle opposite side $AB$ in $\triangle ACB$ ($\triangle ACB$ is same as $\triangle ACB$, so the angle is $\angle C$).
    $$\angle ADE \cong \angle C$$
    Therefore, $\tan(\angle ADE) = \tan(C)$.
3.  **Calculate $\tan(C)$ using $\triangle ABC$:**
    $$\tan(C) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}} = \frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{9}{12} = \frac{3}{4}$$
    Thus, $\tan(\angle ADE) = \frac{3}{4}$.

### Question 7
**Correct Answer: B ($32$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Model the square's diagonal:** The diagonal $d$ of a square with side length $s$ forms two $45^\circ-45^\circ-90^\circ$ special right triangles. The relationship between the side length and the diagonal is:
    $$d = s\sqrt{2}$$
2.  **Find the side length ($s$):**
    $$8\sqrt{2} = s\sqrt{2}$$
    $$s = 8$$
3.  **Calculate the perimeter:** The perimeter of a square is $4s$:
    $$\text{Perimeter} = 4 \times 8 = 32$$

### Question 8
**Correct Answer: C ($20$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Set up the right triangle:** The flagpole (vertical height, $h$) and its shadow (horizontal length, $15$ feet) form a right triangle. The angle of elevation of the sun is $\theta$.
2.  **Apply the tangent ratio:**
    $$\tan(\theta) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Adjacent}} = \frac{h}{15}$$
3.  **Solve for $h$:** Substitute the given value $\tan(\theta) = \frac{4}{3}$:
    $$\frac{4}{3} = \frac{h}{15}$$
    $$h = 15 \times \frac{4}{3} = 20\text{ feet}$$

### Question 9
**Correct Answer: A ($\frac{8}{17}$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Visualize the angle in standard position:** The ray starts at the origin $(0,0)$ and passes through $(8, 15)$ in the first quadrant.
2.  **Form a right triangle:** Drop a perpendicular line from $(8, 15)$ to the $x$-axis.
    *   The adjacent side (horizontal distance $x$) is $8$.
    *   The opposite side (vertical distance $y$) is $15$.
3.  **Find the hypotenuse ($r$):** The distance from the origin to the point $(8,15)$ is:
    $$r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{8^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{64 + 225} = \sqrt{289} = 17$$
4.  **Find $\cos(\theta)$:** By definition:
    $$\cos(\theta) = \frac{x}{r} = \frac{8}{17}$$

### Question 10
**Correct Answer: B ($\frac{4}{5}$)**

**Step-by-step Solution:**
1.  **Decompose the isosceles triangle:** Draw an altitude $BD$ from vertex $B$ perpendicular to base $AC$.
    *   Since $\triangle ABC$ is isosceles with $AB = BC = 10$, the altitude $BD$ bisects the base $AC$.
    *   Thus, $AD = DC = \frac{12}{2} = 6$.
2.  **Find the altitude ($BD$) in right triangle $\triangle ABD$:**
    $$BD^2 + AD^2 = AB^2$$
    $$BD^2 + 6^2 = 10^2$$
    $$BD^2 + 36 = 100$$
    $$BD^2 = 64 \implies BD = 8$$
    *(Note: This is a scaled $3-4-5$ right triangle, specifically a $6-8-10$ triangle).*
3.  **Find $\sin(\angle A)$:** In right triangle $\triangle ABD$:
    $$\sin(\angle A) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}} = \frac{BD}{AB} = \frac{8}{10} = \frac{4}{5}$$
