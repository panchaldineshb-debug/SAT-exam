### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy
In right triangle $ABC$, the right angle is at $C$. If $AC = 5$ and $BC = 12$, what is the value of $\sin(A)$?

A) $\frac{5}{13}$

B) $\frac{12}{13}$

C) $\frac{5}{12}$

D) $\frac{13}{12}$

---

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy
If $\sin(x^\circ) = \cos(2x - 30)^\circ$, where $x$ and $2x - 30$ are acute angles, what is the value of $x$?

A) $20$

B) $30$

C) $40$

D) $50$

---

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy
In a right triangle, one of the acute angles has a measure of $x$ radians, where $\sin(x) = \frac{3}{5}$. What is the value of $\cos(\frac{\pi}{2} - x)$?

A) $\frac{3}{5}$

B) $\frac{4}{5}$

C) $\frac{3}{4}$

D) $\frac{5}{3}$

---

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium
In the figure below, $\triangle ABC$ is a right triangle with a right angle at $B$. If $AC = 10$ and $m\angle A = 60^\circ$, what is the length of side $AB$?

```
   C
   /|
  / |
 /__|
A   B
```

A) $5$

B) $5\sqrt{2}$

C) $5\sqrt{3}$

D) $20$

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

A) $\frac{3}{5}$

B) $\frac{4}{5}$

C) $\frac{3}{4}$

D) $\frac{5}{4}$

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
In the figure below, $AB \perp BC$ and $DE \perp AC$. If $AB = 9$, $BC = 12$, and $AD = 5$, what is the value of $\tan(\angle ADE)$?

```
      C
     /|
    / |
 E /__| D
  /   |
 /____|
A      B
```

A) $\frac{3}{4}$

B) $\frac{4}{3}$

C) $\frac{3}{5}$

D) $\frac{4}{5}$

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
A flagpole casts a shadow that is $15$ feet long when the angle of elevation of the sun is $\theta$. If $\tan(\theta) = \frac{4}{3}$, what is the height of the flagpole, in feet?

A) $11.25$

B) $15$

C) $20$

D) $25$

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

C) $\frac{8}{15}$

D) $\frac{15}{8}$
