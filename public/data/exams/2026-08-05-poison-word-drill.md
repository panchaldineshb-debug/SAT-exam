### Question 1 (Cross-Text Connections)
**Text 1**
For over a century, paleontologists argued that the abrupt extinction of North American megafauna—such as woolly mammoths and saber-toothed cats—around 13,000 years ago was caused exclusively by the "overkill" hypothesis: relentless hunting by newly arrived Clovis human populations. Early kill-site excavations containing fluted projectile points embedded in mammoth bones strongly supported this human-driven narrative.

**Text 2**
While Clovis hunting undoubtedly exerted localized pressure on animal populations, recent paleoclimate reconstructions reveal that the Younger Dryas period triggered abrupt, severe cooling that decimated regional flora. Large herbivores faced sudden collapses in their food supplies before human populations reached significant densities. Attributing the continent-wide extinction solely to human predation ignores the catastrophic ecological disruption caused by climate volatility.

Based on the texts, how would the author of Text 2 most likely respond to the "overkill" hypothesis described in Text 1?
A) By arguing that Clovis hunting had zero measurable impact on any North American megafaunal population.
B) By claiming that climate disruption was entirely responsible for the extinctions and that humans played no role whatsoever.
C) By asserting that early paleontologists completely fabricated the archaeological evidence found at mammoth kill-sites.
D) By suggesting that while human hunting contributed to the decline, climate-driven habitat changes were also a critical factor in the extinction.

Based on the texts, how would the author of Text 2 most likely evaluate the foundational assumption in Text 1?

B) As an entirely fraudulent theory invented solely to deceive corporate regulators.

### Question 3 (Scientific Inference / Cautious Conclusions)

Consequently, the lead investigators emphasized that further phased testing is required before drawing conclusions regarding the drug's therapeutic viability.

Which choice most logically completes the text?
A) proves that beta-amyloid plaques have no biological connection whatsoever to cognitive decline.
