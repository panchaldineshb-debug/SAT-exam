# 40-Minute Advanced Sprint (20 Min Math, 20 Min R&W)

You got it! Since we are jumping straight into the full 40-minute sprint today, make sure you are mentally fresh. 
This is a full Advanced Sprint: 20 minutes for Reading & Writing (14 questions) and 20 minutes for Math (13 questions). 

### 🚨 Core Execution Rules:
1. **The Rounding Ban:** On math grid-ins, keep the long decimal in your calculator until the very final step.
2. **The Rhetorical Synthesis Goal Check:** Before reading the notes, read the question prompt. What *exactly* is the goal?
3. **The Extreme Language Ban:** Treat "entirely," "always," "completely," and "never" as trap indicators in R&W. Look for moderate words like "may", "limitations", or "suggests".
4. **The Negative Sign Check:** When setting up ratios for infinitely many solutions, don't drop your negative signs!
