### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

In triangle ABC, the measure of angle A is 45 degrees, and the measure of angle B is 75 degrees. What is the measure, in degrees, of angle C?

A) 50

B) 60

C) 70

D) 80

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Remember that the sum of the interior angles in any triangle is always 180 degrees.
2. Write an equation using the given angle measures and the unknown angle C: 45 + 75 + C = 180.
3. Simplify the equation by adding the two known angle measures: 120 + C = 180.
4. Subtract 120 from 180 to find the measure of angle C.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

In triangle ABC, side AB is extended past point B to point D. If the measure of the exterior angle CBD is 135 degrees, and the measure of angle CAB is 55 degrees, what is the measure, in degrees, of angle ACB?

A) 45

B) 55

C) 80

D) 90

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall that a straight line forms an angle of 180 degrees. Since ABD is a straight line, the interior angle ABC and the exterior angle CBD are supplementary (they add up to 180 degrees).
2. Find the measure of interior angle ABC by subtracting the exterior angle CBD (135 degrees) from 180.
3. Once you have the measure of angle ABC and are given that angle CAB is 55 degrees, set up an equation where the three interior angles of triangle ABC sum to 180 degrees: CAB + ABC + ACB = 180.
4. Solve for the measure of angle ACB.
5. Alternatively, use the Exterior Angle Theorem, which states that the measure of an exterior angle of a triangle (CBD) is equal to the sum of the measures of its two remote interior angles (CAB + ACB). Set up the equation: 135 = 55 + ACB, and solve for ACB.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

In triangle PQR, PQ = PR. If the measure of angle P is 50 degrees, what is the measure, in degrees, of angle Q?

A) 50

B) 65

C) 80

D) 130

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Note that two sides of the triangle are equal in length: PQ = PR. This means triangle PQR is an isosceles triangle.
2. In an isosceles triangle, the angles opposite the equal sides are also equal in measure. The angle opposite side PQ is angle R, and the angle opposite side PR is angle Q. Therefore, the measure of angle Q is equal to the measure of angle R.
3. Let the measure of angle Q (and angle R) be x.
4. The sum of the angles in triangle PQR is 180 degrees. Write the equation: P + Q + R = 180, which becomes 50 + x + x = 180.
5. Combine like terms to get: 50 + 2x = 180.
6. Solve for x to find the measure of angle Q.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

Lines l and m are parallel. Line segment AC lies on line l, and line segment BD lies on line m. Line segments AB and CD intersect at point E. If the measure of angle CAE is 40 degrees, and the measure of angle BDE is 75 degrees, what is the measure, in degrees, of angle AED?

A) 65

B) 75

C) 105

D) 115

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Since lines l and m are parallel, the alternate interior angles formed by a transversal intersecting them are equal.
2. Identify the transversal line CD. Angle ACE (on line l) and angle BDE (on line m) are alternate interior angles. Therefore, the measure of angle ACE is equal to the measure of angle BDE. Use the given value of BDE to find the measure of angle ACE.
3. Now look at triangle AEC. You know the measures of two of its interior angles: angle CAE (40 degrees) and angle ACE.
4. Recall the Exterior Angle Theorem: an exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the two opposite interior angles. Here, angle AED is an exterior angle to triangle AEC at vertex E, and its opposite interior angles are angle CAE and angle ACE.
5. Calculate the measure of angle AED by adding the measures of angle CAE and angle ACE.
6. (Alternative approach) You can also find the third angle of triangle AEC (angle AEC) since the angles sum to 180 degrees, and then subtract that measure from 180 degrees because angle AEC and angle AED lie on the straight line segment CD and must add up to 180 degrees.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

The measures of the three interior angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2:3:5. What is the measure, in degrees, of the largest angle of the triangle?

A) 54

B) 72

C) 90

D) 100

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Represent the measures of the three angles in terms of a variable x based on the given ratio 2:3:5. The angles can be written as 2x, 3x, and 5x.
2. The sum of the interior angles of a triangle is 180 degrees. Set up an equation: 2x + 3x + 5x = 180.
3. Simplify the equation by combining the x terms: 10x = 180.
4. Solve for x by dividing both sides of the equation by 10.
5. Identify which of the three expressions (2x, 3x, or 5x) represents the largest angle.
6. Multiply the value of x by the coefficient of the largest angle expression to find its measure in degrees.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

Triangle ABC is equilateral and shares side AC with right triangle ACD, where the right angle is at C. If the length of side AC is equal to the length of side CD, what is the measure, in degrees, of angle BAD?

A) 75

B) 90

C) 105

D) 120

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Since triangle ABC is equilateral, all of its interior angles are equal to 60 degrees. Identify the measure of angle BAC.
2. Look at triangle ACD. It is given as a right triangle with the right angle at C, meaning angle ACD = 90 degrees.
3. You are also given that side AC is equal in length to side CD. This makes triangle ACD an isosceles right triangle.
4. In an isosceles triangle, the angles opposite the equal sides are equal. In triangle ACD, the angles opposite AC and CD are angle CDA and angle CAD, respectively. Therefore, angle CAD = angle CDA.
5. Set up an equation for the angles in triangle ACD: CAD + CDA + ACD = 180. Since CAD = CDA and ACD = 90, this becomes 2 * (CAD) + 90 = 180. Solve for angle CAD.
6. The total angle BAD is the sum of adjacent angles BAC and CAD. Add the measures of angle BAC and angle CAD to find the measure of angle BAD.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium

In triangle XYZ, the measure of angle X is (2x + 15) degrees, the measure of angle Y is (3x - 10) degrees, and the measure of angle Z is (4x + 22) degrees. What is the measure, in degrees, of the smallest angle of the triangle?

A) 17

B) 41

C) 49

D) 90

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Remember that the sum of the three interior angles of a triangle is always 180 degrees.
2. Set up an equation summing the three algebraic expressions for angles X, Y, and Z to 180: (2x + 15) + (3x - 10) + (4x + 22) = 180.
3. Combine the like terms (the terms with x, and the constant numbers) on the left side of the equation.
4. Solve the simplified equation to find the value of x.
5. Substitute this value of x back into each of the original expressions: (2x + 15), (3x - 10), and (4x + 22) to determine the measure of each of the three angles in degrees.
6. Compare the three resulting angle measures to identify the smallest one.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Medium

In triangle ABC, the lengths of the sides are AB = 8, BC = 12, and AC = 10. Which of the following lists the interior angles of the triangle in order from least to greatest measure?

A) angle A, angle B, angle C

B) angle B, angle C, angle A

C) angle C, angle B, angle A

D) angle C, angle A, angle B

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the geometric relationship between the side lengths of a triangle and the measures of its opposite angles: the shortest side is always opposite the smallest angle, and the longest side is always opposite the largest angle.
2. Identify which angle is opposite each side of triangle ABC:
   - The angle opposite side AB is angle C.
   - The angle opposite side BC is angle A.
   - The angle opposite side AC is angle B.
3. Order the side lengths from shortest to longest (least to greatest): AB = 8, AC = 10, BC = 12.
4. Write an inequality showing the order of the sides: AB < AC < BC.
5. Replace each side in the inequality with its opposite angle to find the correct order of the angles from least to greatest.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

Line segments AD and BC intersect at point E. Line segment AB connects points A and B, and line segment CD connects points C and D, forming triangles ABE and CDE. If the measure of angle A is 80 degrees, the measure of angle B is 45 degrees, and the measure of angle D is 55 degrees, what is the measure, in degrees, of angle C?

A) 45

B) 55

C) 70

D) 80

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Identify the two intersecting line segments: AD and BC, which meet at point E. This intersection creates vertical angles: angle AEB and angle CED.
2. Recall that vertical angles are always equal in measure. So, the measure of angle AEB is equal to the measure of angle CED.
3. In triangle ABE, you are given the measures of angle A (80 degrees) and angle B (45 degrees). Use the fact that the sum of angles in a triangle is 180 degrees to calculate the measure of the third angle, angle AEB.
4. Using the vertical angles property from Step 2, the measure of angle CED is equal to the measure of angle AEB you just found.
5. In triangle CDE, you now know the measure of angle CED and are given the measure of angle D (55 degrees). Use the angle sum of a triangle (180 degrees) to solve for the remaining angle, angle C.
6. (Alternative shortcut) Observe that since vertical angles are equal, the sum of the other two angles in one triangle must equal the sum of the other two angles in the second triangle: angle A + angle B = angle C + angle D. Substitute the known values and solve for angle C.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

Quadrilateral ABCD has diagonal BD. Triangle ABD is an isosceles triangle where AB = AD and the measure of angle BAD is 100 degrees. Triangle BCD is a right triangle with the right angle at C. If the measure of angle CBD is 35 degrees, what is the measure, in degrees, of angle ADC?

A) 75

B) 85

C) 95

D) 105

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. The figure consists of two adjacent triangles, triangle ABD and triangle BCD, which share the side BD. The target angle, angle ADC, is composed of two adjacent angles: angle ADB and angle BDC. Therefore, angle ADC = angle ADB + angle BDC.
2. Focus on triangle ABD first. You are given that AB = AD, which means it is an isosceles triangle. The vertex angle BAD is 100 degrees.
3. In an isosceles triangle, the base angles are equal. So, the measure of angle ADB is equal to the measure of angle ABD.
4. Set up an equation for triangle ABD: BAD + ADB + ABD = 180. Since ADB = ABD, this is 100 + 2 * (ADB) = 180. Solve for angle ADB.
5. Now focus on triangle BCD. It is a right triangle with the right angle at C (angle BCD = 90 degrees), and you are given that angle CBD is 35 degrees.
6. Find the measure of angle BDC by using the sum of angles in triangle BCD: BCD + CBD + BDC = 180, which is 90 + 35 + BDC = 180. Solve for angle BDC.
7. Finally, add the measures of angle ADB (from Step 4) and angle BDC (from Step 6) to find the total measure of angle ADC.
