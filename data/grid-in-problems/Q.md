# SAT Prep: Grid In Problems - Practice Questions (Q.md)

This file contains 10 practice grid-in questions for the SAT. Each question includes a description followed by hints and guided steps — **no answers or multiple-choice options are given**. Work through the hints to find the numerical solution yourself, Student!

---

### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

If 3x + 7 = 22, what is the value of x?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Isolate the variable term `3x` by subtracting 7 from both sides of the equation: `3x = 22 - 7`.
2. Simplify the right side: `3x = 15`.
3. Solve for `x` by dividing both sides by 3.

---

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

A local bakery sells chocolate chip cookies for $1.50 each. If a customer spends a total of $18.00 on these cookies, how many cookies did the customer buy?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Set up an equation representing the situation: `Cost per cookie * Number of cookies = Total spent`.
2. Substitute the given values: `1.50 * x = 18.00`, where `x` is the number of cookies.
3. Solve for `x` by dividing 18.00 by 1.50. (Hint: you can multiply both numbers by 10 or 2 to make the division easier: `180 / 15` or `36 / 3`).

---

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

In a class of 24 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 3:5. How many girls are in the class?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Express the ratio of boys to girls in terms of parts: there are 3 parts boys and 5 parts girls.
2. Find the total number of parts by adding the ratio terms: `3 + 5 = 8 parts`.
3. Determine the size of one single part by dividing the total number of students by the total parts: `24 students / 8 parts`.
4. Find the number of girls by multiplying the size of one part by the number of parts representing girls (which is 5).

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Easy

If x^2 - 9 = 0 and x > 0, what is the value of x?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Isolate the `x^2` term by adding 9 to both sides of the equation: `x^2 = 9`.
2. Solve for `x` by taking the square root of both sides.
3. Keep only the positive solution since the question states that `x > 0`.

---

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

The average (arithmetic mean) of five numbers is 12. If four of the numbers are 8, 15, 10, and 14, what is the fifth number?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the average formula: `Average = Sum of all values / Number of values`.
2. Set up the equation using the given values: `(8 + 15 + 10 + 14 + x) / 5 = 12`, where `x` is the fifth number.
3. Multiply both sides by 5 to find the total sum of the five numbers: `Sum = 12 * 5`.
4. Add the four known numbers together: `8 + 15 + 10 + 14`.
5. Subtract this combined sum from the total sum found in Step 3 to solve for `x`.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

If \sqrt{2x - 3} = 5, what is the value of x?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Eliminate the radical sign by squaring both sides of the equation: `(\sqrt{2x - 3})^2 = 5^2`.
2. Simplify both sides: `2x - 3 = 25`.
3. Isolate the variable term `2x` by adding 3 to both sides: `2x = 25 + 3`.
4. Solve for `x` by dividing both sides by 2.

---

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium

A rectangular garden has an area of 120 square feet. If the length of the garden is 15 feet, what is the perimeter, in feet, of the garden?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the area formula for a rectangle: `Area = Length * Width`.
2. Substitute the given values to find the width: `120 = 15 * Width`. Solve for `Width`.
3. Recall the perimeter formula for a rectangle: `Perimeter = 2 * Length + 2 * Width` (or `2 * (Length + Width)`).
4. Substitute the length (15) and the calculated width into the perimeter formula to calculate the final answer.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Hard

If 2^x * 2^(x + 3) = 32, what is the value of x?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Apply the product rule of exponents to combine the terms on the left side: `2^a * 2^b = 2^(a + b)`.
2. Combine the exponents: `2^(x + x + 3) = 2^(2x + 3)`.
3. Express the right side of the equation with the same base: rewrite `32` as a power of 2 (which is `2^5`).
4. Set up the new equation: `2^(2x + 3) = 2^5`.
5. Since the bases are the same, equate the exponents: `2x + 3 = 5`.
6. Solve the linear equation for `x`.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

In the xy-plane, a line passes through the origin (0, 0) and the point (4, 18). What is the slope of the line? (Write your answer as a decimal).

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the slope formula: `Slope = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1)`.
2. Substitute the coordinates of the two points: `(0, 0)` and `(4, 18)`.
3. Set up the fraction: `(18 - 0) / (4 - 0) = 18 / 4`.
4. Simplify the fraction and write it in its decimal form (since SAT grid-in allows decimals).

---

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

If a + b = 9 and a - b = 3, what is the value of a^2 - b^2?

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the difference of squares factoring identity: `a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)`.
2. Notice that the values for `a + b` and `a - b` are already given in the problem as 9 and 3, respectively.
3. Substitute these values directly into the factored identity: `(9) * (3)`.
4. Multiply to find the final result.
