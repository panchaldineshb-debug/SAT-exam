# SAT Prep: Unit Rate Problems - Practice Questions (Q.md)

This file contains 10 practice questions on Unit Rate Problems for the SAT. Each question includes a multiple-choice layout followed by hints and guided steps — **no answers are given**. Work through the hints to find the solution yourself, Student!

---

### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

A car travels 180 miles in 3 hours. At this constant rate, how many miles will it travel in 5 hours?

A) 240

B) 270

C) 300

D) 360

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the unit rate of speed (miles per hour) by dividing the total distance by the total time: `180 miles / 3 hours`.
2. Once you have the speed in miles per hour, multiply this rate by the target time of 5 hours to find the new distance.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

A machine produces 240 widgets in 8 minutes. What is the production rate of the machine in widgets per second?

A) 0.5

B) 2

C) 30

D) 120

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. First, find the rate of production in widgets per minute by dividing: `240 widgets / 8 minutes`.
2. Recall that there are 60 seconds in 1 minute.
3. Convert the rate from widgets per minute to widgets per second by dividing the widgets-per-minute rate by 60.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

If a 12-ounce bottle of shampoo costs $4.80, what is the unit price of the shampoo in dollars per ounce?

A) $0.30

B) $0.40

C) $0.50

D) $2.50

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. To find the cost per ounce, divide the total cost by the total number of ounces: `$4.80 / 12 ounces`.
2. Perform the division to find the price of one single ounce.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

A printer prints pages at a constant rate of 40 pages per minute. How many seconds will it take the printer to print a document that is 150 pages long?

A) 125

B) 225

C) 240

D) 375

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Calculate the time needed in minutes by dividing the total pages by the pages printed per minute: `150 pages / 40 pages per minute`.
2. Convert this time from minutes to seconds by multiplying the result by 60 (since there are 60 seconds in a minute).

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

A cyclist travels at a constant speed of 15 miles per hour. What is the cyclist's speed in feet per second? (1 mile = 5,280 feet)

A) 10

B) 22

C) 44

D) 88

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Set up a dimensional analysis conversion. Start with `15 miles / 1 hour`.
2. Convert miles to feet: multiply by `5,280 feet / 1 mile`. This gives `15 * 5,280` feet per hour.
3. Convert hours to seconds: divide by 3,600 (since 1 hour = 60 minutes, and 1 minute = 60 seconds, so 1 hour = 3,600 seconds).
4. Perform the calculation: `(15 * 5,280) / 3,600`.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

A swimming pool is being filled with water at a constant rate of 12 gallons per minute. The pool has a total capacity of 3,600 gallons and already contains 600 gallons of water. How many hours will it take to fill the pool completely?

A) 4.17

B) 5.0

C) 250.0

D) 300.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Calculate the remaining volume of water needed to fill the pool: `Total Capacity - Current Water = 3,600 - 600`.
2. Find the time needed in minutes by dividing the remaining volume by the fill rate: `Remaining Volume / 12 gallons per minute`.
3. Convert the time from minutes to hours by dividing the result by 60.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium

If 3 workers can pack 180 boxes in 4 hours working at a constant rate, how many boxes can 5 workers pack in 6 hours working at the same rate?

A) 225

B) 300

C) 375

D) 450

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the rate of packing for one single worker.
   - First, find the rate of 3 workers: `180 boxes / 4 hours = 45 boxes per hour` (for all 3 workers combined).
   - Divide this combined rate by the number of workers (3) to find the rate per worker: `45 / 3 = 15 boxes per hour per worker`.
2. Calculate the rate of 5 workers: multiply the single-worker rate by 5.
3. Multiply the 5-worker rate by the target time of 6 hours to find the total boxes packed.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Hard

A solid metal bar has a mass of 4.5 kilograms and a volume of 500 cubic centimeters. What is the density of the metal in grams per cubic millimeter? (1 kilogram = 1,000 grams; 1 cubic centimeter = 1,000 cubic millimeters)

A) 0.009

B) 0.09

C) 9.0

D) 90.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the density formula: `Density = Mass / Volume`.
2. Convert the mass from kilograms to grams: `4.5 kilograms * 1,000 grams per kilogram = 4,500 grams`.
3. Convert the volume from cubic centimeters to cubic millimeters: `500 cubic centimeters * 1,000 cubic millimeters per cubic centimeter = 500,000 cubic millimeters`.
4. Divide the mass in grams by the volume in cubic millimeters: `4,500 / 500,000`.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

A delivery truck travels at a constant speed of 60 miles per hour for the first 120 miles of a trip, and then at a constant speed of 40 miles per hour for the next 80 miles. What is the average speed of the truck, in miles per hour, for the entire trip?

A) 48

B) 50

C) 52

D) 55

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall that average speed is calculated as: `Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time`.
2. Find the total distance of the trip: `120 miles + 80 miles`.
3. Find the time spent on the first part of the trip: `120 miles / 60 miles per hour`.
4. Find the time spent on the second part of the trip: `80 miles / 40 miles per hour`.
5. Sum the times from Step 3 and Step 4 to find the total time of the trip.
6. Divide the total distance by the total time.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

Water flows into a tank at a rate of 5 gallons per minute, but leaks out of a hole in the bottom of the tank at a rate of 1.5 gallons per minute. If the tank is initially empty and has a capacity of 700 gallons, how many hours and minutes will it take to fill the tank completely?

A) 2 hours and 20 minutes

B) 3 hours and 20 minutes

C) 3 hours and 33 minutes

D) 4 hours and 40 minutes

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Find the net rate at which water is accumulating in the tank: `Inflow Rate - Leakage Rate = 5 - 1.5` gallons per minute.
2. Divide the total capacity of the tank (700 gallons) by the net rate to find the total time in minutes.
3. Convert the total time in minutes to hours and minutes (by dividing by 60 to find the whole number of hours, and keeping the remainder as minutes).
