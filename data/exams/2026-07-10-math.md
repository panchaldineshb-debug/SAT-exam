### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

What is the value of x in the solution to the system of equations below?
x + 2y = 10
3x - 2y = 6

A) 2

B) 4

C) 6

D) 8

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Notice that the y-terms in the two equations have opposite coefficients: +2y and -2y.
2. Because they are opposites, you can add the two equations together to eliminate the y-variable: (x + 2y) + (3x - 2y) = 10 + 6.
3. Simplify the resulting equation by combining like terms: 4x = 16.
4. Solve for x by dividing both sides by 4.

2. Substitute x = 3 and y = 2 into the second equation: a(3) - 2(2) = 8.

4. Solve for a by first adding 4 to both sides, and then dividing by 3.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

In the xy-plane, the system of equations below has no solution. What is the value of k?
y = -2x + 7
y = kx - 3

A) -3

B) -2

C) 3

D) 7

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Remember that a system of two linear equations in the xy-plane has no solution if and only if the lines are parallel.
2. Parallel lines have the same slope but different y-intercepts.
3. Identify the slope of the first line y = -2x + 7 (which is the coefficient of x, so it is -2).
4. Identify the slope of the second line y = kx - 3 (which is k).
5. Set the two slopes equal to find the value of k.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

At a café, 2 coffees and 3 muffins cost $14.50, and 4 coffees and 1 muffin cost $14.00. What is the cost of 1 muffin?

A) $1.50

B) $2.00

C) $3.00

D) $3.50

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Define your variables: let c be the cost of one coffee and m be the cost of one muffin.
2. Translate the word problem into two equations:
   - 2c + 3m = 14.50
   - 4c + m = 14.00
3. To solve for m, it is easiest to eliminate c. Compare the c-coefficients (2 and 4).
4. Multiply the first equation by 2: 2 * (2c + 3m) = 2 * 14.50, which simplifies to 4c + 6m = 29.00.
5. Subtract the second equation from this new equation: (4c + 6m) - (4c + m) = 29.00 - 14.00.
6. Simplify to get: 5m = 15.00.
7. Divide by 5 to find the value of m.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

If 4x + y = 17 and x + 4y = 13, what is the value of x + y?

A) 3

B) 4

C) 6

D) 30

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Look at the coefficients of x and y in both equations: they are symmetric (4 and 1, 1 and 4).
2. Instead of solving for x and y individually, you can find x + y directly by adding the two equations together.
3. Add the left sides and the right sides: (4x + y) + (x + 4y) = 17 + 13.
4. Combine like terms: 5x + 5y = 30.
5. Factor out 5 on the left side: 5(x + y) = 30.
6. Divide both sides by 5 to find the value of x + y.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

In the system of equations below, c is a constant. If the system has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of c?
y = 2x - 3
4x - 2y = c

A) -6

B) -3

C) 3

D) 6

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. A system of two linear equations has infinitely many solutions if they represent the exact same line.
2. First, rewrite the first equation in standard form (Ax + By = C) to make it easier to compare. Subtract 2x from both sides of y = 2x - 3 to get: -2x + y = -3.
3. Now look at the second equation: 4x - 2y = c.
4. Compare the coefficients of x and y between the two equations:
   - For x: from -2 to 4 (multiplied by -2)
   - For y: from 1 to -2 (multiplied by -2)
5. Since the left side of the second equation is exactly the first equation's left side multiplied by -2, the right side must also be multiplied by -2.
6. Multiply the constant term of the first equation (-3) by -2 to find the value of c.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**

2. Set up the proportion for the coefficients: a / 3 = -5 / -2.
3. Simplify the fraction on the right side: a / 3 = 5/2.
4. Solve for a by multiplying both sides of the equation by 3.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Hard

The graphs of the equations 3x - y = 5 and ax + 2y = 8 intersect at a point on the x-axis. What is the value of the constant a?

A) 2.4

B) 3.2

C) 4.8

D) 5.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. A point on the x-axis always has a y-coordinate of 0. Let the intersection point be (x, 0).
2. Since this point lies on the line 3x - y = 5, substitute y = 0 into this equation to find the x-coordinate of the intersection point: 3x - 0 = 5.
3. Solve for x: x = 5/3.
4. Now you know the intersection point is (5/3, 0). This point must also lie on the second line ax + 2y = 8.
5. Substitute x = 5/3 and y = 0 into ax + 2y = 8 to get: a(5/3) + 2(0) = 8.
6. Simplify and solve for a: a(5/3) = 8. Multiply both sides by 3/5 to isolate a.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

A chemist wants to mix a 10% saline solution and a 25% saline solution to obtain 30 liters of a 15% saline solution. How many liters of the 10% saline solution should be used?

A) 10

B) 15

C) 20

D) 25

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Define the variables: let x be the number of liters of the 10% solution, and y be the number of liters of the 25% solution.
2. Write the first equation for the total volume of liquid: x + y = 30.
3. Write the second equation for the total amount of pure salt: 0.10x + 0.25y = 0.15 * 30.
4. Simplify the second equation: 0.10x + 0.25y = 4.5.
5. To clear decimals, you can multiply the entire second equation by 100: 10x + 25y = 450.
6. Now you have a system:
   - x + y = 30
   - 10x + 25y = 450
7. The question asks for the volume of the 10% solution, which is x. So, eliminate y.
8. Multiply the first equation by 25: 25x + 25y = 750.
9. Subtract the equation 10x + 25y = 450 from 25x + 25y = 750.
10. Solve the resulting equation for x.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

If 5x - 3y = 14 and 2x + y = 10, what is the value of 3x - 4y?

A) 4

B) 6

C) 8

D) 24

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Examine the expression you want to find: 3x - 4y.
2. Look at the coefficients of x and y in the given equations: (5 and 2) and (-3 and 1).
3. Try subtracting the second equation from the first: (5x - 3y) - (2x + y) = 14 - 10.
4. Simplify the left side: 5x - 3y - 2x - y = 3x - 4y.
5. Simplify the right side: 14 - 10 = 4.
6. Notice that the simplified left side is exactly the expression we are trying to evaluate. Therefore, the value of 3x - 4y is equal to the simplified right side.

### Question 15
**Difficulty:** Medium

A group of 40 people consists of adults and children. Tickets for adults cost $12 and tickets for children cost $8. If the total ticket sales were $380, how many adults were in the group?

A) 15

B) 20

C) 25

D) 30

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let A be the number of adults and C be the number of children.
2. Write an equation representing the total number of people: A + C = 40.
3. Write an equation representing the total ticket cost: 12A + 8C = 380.
4. To solve for the number of adults (A), express C in terms of A using the first equation: C = 40 - A.
5. Substitute this expression for C into the cost equation: 12A + 8(40 - A) = 380.
6. Distribute and solve for A: 12A + 320 - 8A = 380. Combine like terms and isolate A.

### Question 16

In the xy-plane, the line y = mx + b has a slope of 3 and passes through the intersection point of the lines y = 2x + 1 and y = -x + 7. What is the value of b?

D) 8

**Hint / Guided Steps:**

2. Set the two equations equal to each other to solve for x: 2x + 1 = -x + 7.
3. Add x to both sides and subtract 1 from both sides to get: 3x = 6. Solve for x.
4. Find y by substituting the x-value back into y = 2x + 1.
5. Now you have the coordinates of the intersection point (x, y).
6. Substitute this point and the slope m = 3 into the equation y = mx + b (or y = 3x + b) to solve for the y-intercept, b.

### Question 17
**Difficulty:** Hard

If the system of equations below has infinitely many solutions, what is the value of k?
4x - 6y = 10
6x - ky = 15

A) 4

B) 6

C) 9

D) 12

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. For the system to have infinitely many solutions, one equation must be a constant multiple of the other.
2. Compare the constants on the right side: 10 and 15. The second equation's constant is 1.5 times the first (15 / 10 = 1.5).
3. Check the x-coefficients: the first is 4, the second is 6. Notice that 4 * 1.5 = 6, which matches.
4. Therefore, the y-coefficient in the second equation (-k) must be 1.5 times the y-coefficient in the first equation (-6).
5. Set up the equation: -k = -6 * 1.5.
6. Solve for k.

### Question 19
**Difficulty:** Hard

A system of equations is shown below:
2x - y = 5
ax - 3y = b
If the system has no solution, which of the following could be the values of a and b?

A) a = 6, b = 15

B) a = 6, b = 10

C) a = 2, b = 15

D) a = 2, b = 5

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. For a system of two linear equations to have no solution, the lines must be parallel. This means they have the same slope but different y-intercepts.
2. In standard form (Ax + By = C), the coefficients of x and y must be proportional: A1 / A2 = B1 / B2.
3. For our equations, this means: 2 / a = -1 / -3.
4. Simplify the ratio: 2 / a = 1 / 3. Solve this to find the required value of a.
5. To ensure there is no solution (rather than infinitely many solutions), the constant terms must not be in the same proportion: 5 / b must not equal 1 / 3.
6. Calculate what value of b would make the lines identical (infinitely many solutions), and make sure b is NOT that value.
7. Choose the option that matches the required value of a but has a value of b that does not make the equations identical.

### Question 20
**Difficulty:** Hard

In a two-digit number, the sum of the digits is 11. If the digits are reversed, the new number is 45 less than the original number. What is the original number?

A) 83

B) 74

C) 65

D) 56

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let t be the tens digit and u be the units digit of the original number.
2. The value of the original number is 10t + u.
3. Write the first equation for the sum of the digits: t + u = 11.
4. When the digits are reversed, the new number's value is 10u + t.
5. Write the second equation expressing that the new number is 45 less than the original number: (10u + t) = (10t + u) - 45.
6. Simplify the second equation by moving variables to one side: 9t - 9u = 45.
7. Divide the entire second equation by 9 to simplify it: t - u = 5.
8. Now you have a simple system of equations:
   - t + u = 11
   - t - u = 5
9. Solve this system to find the digits t and u.
10. Combine the digits to write the original number (10t + u).
