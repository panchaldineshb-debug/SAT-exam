# 20-Minute Trap Resolution Sprint: Reading & Writing and Math

Welcome back, Student! 
This sprint is specifically designed to test whether you have mastered the 7 traps we identified from your last session. You have **20 minutes for Reading & Writing** (14 questions) and **20 minutes for Math** (12 questions). 

### 🚨 Core Execution Rules (The Trap-Breaker Protocol):
1. **Target Check:** Double-check *exactly* what the math question is asking for before selecting an answer (e.g., did it ask for $x$, or did it ask for the maximum height?).
2. **The "Who is Doing It?" Rule:** For dangling modifiers, make sure the noun immediately following the comma is the one actually performing the action.
3. **Appositives & Lists:** Remember the deletion test for names, and check for semicolon consistency in complex lists.
4. **Base Conversion:** Never equate exponents until the bases are identical.

## 📐 Section 2: Math (20 Minutes, 12 Questions)
*Focus: Target Checks, Exponential Decay, Base Conversions, and Advanced Algebra.*

### Question 15 (The Target Check Trap - Vertex)
A model rocket is launched from a platform. Its height $h(t)$, in meters, after $t$ seconds is modeled by the function $h(t) = -5t^2 + 40t + 15$. What is the **maximum height**, in meters, reached by the rocket?
A) 4
B) 15
C) 95
D) 175

### Question 16 (The Exponential Decay Trap)
The population of a specific bacteria colony in a lab is modeled by the function $P(t) = 8000(0.25)^{\frac{t}{6}}$, where $t$ is the time in hours. Which of the following best describes the behavior of the bacteria population?
A) The population decreases by 25% every 6 hours.
B) The population decreases by 75% every 6 hours.
C) The population decreases by 25% every hour.
D) The population decreases by 6% every 25 hours.

### Question 17 (The Base Conversion Trap)
If $9^{x+2} = 27^{x-1}$, what is the value of $x$?
A) 3
B) 4
C) 5
D) 7

### Question 18 (Target Check - Word Problem)
A recipe for a large batch of fruit punch requires a ratio of 5 parts orange juice to 3 parts pineapple juice. If a caterer makes a batch of punch that contains a total of 120 liters of juice, how many liters of **orange juice** are in the batch?
A) 45
B) 75
C) 120
D) 200

### Question 19 (Advanced Algebra - Polynomials)
If the expression $\frac{4x^2 + 6x - 5}{2x + 1}$ is written in the form $2x + A + \frac{B}{2x + 1}$, where $A$ and $B$ are constants, what is the value of $B$?
A) 2
B) -5
C) -7
D) 7

### Question 20 (Target Check - Systems of Equations)

B) -1

D) 4
