### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Domain:** Standard English Conventions (Form, Structure, and Sense)

By the time the museum curator unpacked the ancient pottery, the conservation team ______ the necessary display cases.

A) has constructed

B) will construct

C) had constructed

D) construct

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Identify the relationship between the two actions in the sentence: "unpacked" and "constructed."
2. The word "unpacked" is in the simple past tense. 
3. The phrase "By the time [past action]" indicates that the construction of the display cases was completed *before* the unpacking occurred.
4. Recall which verb tense is used to describe an action that was completed before another past event.
5. Select the option that uses this past perfect tense.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Domain:** Standard English Conventions (Form, Structure, and Sense)

By the end of this month, the software developers ______ the new update to all beta testers.

A) will have released

B) released

C) release

D) had released

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Look at the time marker: "By the end of this month." This refers to a future deadline.
2. The sentence describes an action that will be completed by/before this future point.
3. Recall that the future perfect tense is used to express an action that will be finished at or before a specified time in the future.
4. Find the option that matches the future perfect tense structure (*will have + past participle*).

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Domain:** Standard English Conventions (Form, Structure, and Sense)

Since the city council implemented the new recycling guidelines last year, the local landfill ______ a significant reduction in plastic waste.

A) experienced

B) has experienced

C) experiences

D) will experience

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Locate the time marker in the dependent clause: "Since the city council implemented..."
2. The word "Since" indicates that the action started at a point in the past (last year) and has continued up to the present.
3. Recall that the present perfect tense is used for an action that began in the past and continues to or has relevance in the present.
4. Select the present perfect form of the verb (*has/have + past participle*).

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Domain:** Standard English Conventions (Form, Structure, and Sense)

If the research team had secured funding earlier, they ______ the clinical trials by now.

A) would have completed

B) will complete

C) completed

D) complete

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Examine the structure of the conditional sentence: "If the research team had secured..."
2. The clause starting with "If" uses the past perfect tense ("had secured"). This indicates a hypothetical condition in the past (a third conditional structure).
3. The result clause of a third conditional sentence must use the structure: *would have + past participle*.
4. Choose the option that completes the conditional sentence correctly.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Domain:** Standard English Conventions (Form, Structure, and Sense)

Over the past three decades, climatologists ______ temperature fluctuations in the Arctic to better understand global climate patterns.

A) monitor

B) had monitored

C) have been monitoring

D) will monitor

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Look at the duration marker: "Over the past three decades."
2. This indicates an action that started thirty years ago and is still ongoing in the present.
3. Recall that the present perfect continuous tense is used to show that an action started in the past and has continued up to the present.
4. Select the present perfect continuous form (*has/have been + verbing*).
