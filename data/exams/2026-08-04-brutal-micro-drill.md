## ⏱️ Section 1: Reading & Writing (3 Questions)

### Question 1 (Extreme Language - Cross Text)
**Text 1**
For decades, marine biologists believed that the decline of coral reefs in the Caribbean was caused exclusively by nutrient runoff from agricultural fertilizers, which promoted algal blooms that smothered living polyps. Early studies consistently showed high nitrogen and phosphorus concentrations in areas with the most severe reef degradation.

**Text 2**
While agricultural runoff undoubtedly damages coral ecosystems, subsequent research has demonstrated that overfishing of herbivorous fish species—such as parrotfish—is an equally critical factor. Without these herbivores grazing on algae, even moderate nutrient levels allow algae to proliferate uncontrollably. Attributing the crisis to a single industrial cause oversimplifies a complex ecological breakdown.

Based on the texts, how would the author of Text 2 most likely respond to the claims of the early studies cited in Text 1?
A) By arguing that agricultural runoff has never had a measurable effect on Caribbean coral reefs.
B) By claiming that overfishing is entirely responsible for the Caribbean coral crisis and fertilizers play no role.
C) By asserting that early researchers completely fabricated their nitrogen and phosphorus data.
D) By suggesting that while agricultural runoff contributes to coral decline, it does not fully account for the severity of the crisis.

### Question 2 (Extreme Language - Argument Evaluation)
**Text 1**
Advocates of remote work policies frequently point to corporate surveys indicating a 12% rise in self-reported worker satisfaction and reduced turnover. They maintain that remote flexibility is an unqualified improvement across all organizational functions.

**Text 2**
Worker satisfaction surveys conducted during initial policy transitions often reflect a temporary "honeymoon" phase. Longitudinal tracking across software and design teams reveals that spontaneous cross-departmental collaboration dropped by 34% over a two-year period, leading to slower product iteration cycles and increased onboarding friction for junior staff.

Based on the texts, how would the author of Text 2 most likely evaluate the claim made by the advocates in Text 1?
A) As a perspective that overlooks long-term collaborative and developmental challenges in certain roles.
B) As an entirely fraudulent claim designed to deceive corporate executives.
C) As proof that remote work always leads to complete organizational failure within two years.
D) As a finding that should prompt companies to permanently eliminate all flexible work options.

### Question 3 (Rhetorical Synthesis - Goal Precision)
While researching a topic, a student has taken the following notes:
* The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) uses infrared instruments to observe deep space objects.
* The Hubble Space Telescope primarily observes in optical and ultraviolet wavelengths.
* Because infrared light penetrates cosmic dust clouds, JWST can image stars and planetary systems forming inside dense nebulae.
* Hubble’s optical sensors are frequently blocked by dense cosmic dust clouds.
* Both telescopes have provided revolutionary images of distant galaxies.

The student wants to **emphasize a specific technological advantage of the JWST over the Hubble Telescope**. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to accomplish this goal?
A) While both telescopes have provided revolutionary images of deep space, Hubble observes in optical and ultraviolet wavelengths.
B) JWST's infrared instruments allow it to peer through dense cosmic dust clouds to image forming stars, a capability Hubble's optical sensors lack due to dust blockage.
C) The James Webb Space Telescope is an entirely superior observatory that has rendered the Hubble Space Telescope completely obsolete.
D) Unlike Hubble, which primarily observes in optical wavelengths, JWST uses infrared instruments to take revolutionary images of distant galaxies.
