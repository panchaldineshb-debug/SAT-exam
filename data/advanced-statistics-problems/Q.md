### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

A survey of 400 randomly selected residents of a town was conducted to determine support for a new community park. The survey found that 60% of those surveyed supported the park. What is the sample size of the survey?

A) 60

B) 240

C) 400

D) 600

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the definition of sample size: the sample size is the total number of individuals or items selected and analyzed in a study.
2. Read the prompt to identify the total number of residents selected for the survey.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

A dataset consists of test scores. The scores are grouped into intervals and represented in a histogram. The intervals and frequencies are:
- Scores 60–69: 3 students
- Scores 70–79: 8 students
- Scores 80–89: 12 students
- Scores 90–100: 7 students
In which score interval does the median score lie?

A) 60–69

B) 70–79

C) 80–89

D) 90–100

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Determine the total number of students in the dataset by adding the frequencies: `3 + 8 + 12 + 7 = 30` students.
2. Identify the position of the median. Since there are 30 students (an even number), the median is the average of the 15th and 16th scores when arranged in order.
3. Find which interval contains the 15th and 16th scores by calculating cumulative frequencies:
   - First interval (60-69) contains scores 1 to 3.
   - Second interval (70-79) contains scores 4 to 11 (`3 + 8 = 11`).
   - Third interval (80-89) contains scores 12 to 23 (`11 + 12 = 23`).
4. Identify which interval covers the 15th and 16th positions.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

Class A has 20 students with an average exam score of 80. Class B has 30 students with an average exam score of 90. If the two classes are combined, what is the average score of the combined group?

A) 84.0

B) 85.0

C) 86.0

D) 88.0

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. To find the combined average (weighted mean), calculate the total sum of all scores for both classes combined, and divide by the total number of students.
2. Calculate the sum of scores for Class A: `20 students * 80 average score = 1,600`.
3. Calculate the sum of scores for Class B: `30 students * 90 average score = 2,700`.
4. Add these sums together to find the total combined score: `1,600 + 2,700 = 4,300`.
5. Find the total number of students: `20 + 30 = 50`.
6. Divide the total combined score by the total number of students: `4,300 / 50`.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

A study of a random sample of 500 students at a school found that 40% of them play a musical instrument. The margin of error for this estimate is 3%. Which of the following is a plausible value for the actual percentage of all students at the school who play a musical instrument?

A) 35%

B) 38%

C) 44%

D) 45%

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Understand the meaning of margin of error: it represents the range around the sample estimate in which the true population parameter is expected to fall.
2. Calculate the lower and upper bounds of this range using the sample estimate (40%) and the margin of error (3%):
   - Lower bound = `40% - 3% = 37%`.
   - Upper bound = `40% + 3% = 43%`.
3. The true percentage is likely between 37% and 43% (inclusive).
4. Review the options to find which value falls inside this interval.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

A researcher wants to determine the average amount of time high school students in a district spend on homework each night. Which of the following sampling methods is most likely to produce a representative, unbiased sample?

A) Surveying all students in an advanced placement chemistry class.

B) Surveying 100 students selected at random from the school district directory.

C) Surveying the first 100 students who enter the school library in the morning.

D) Surveying all members of the school varsity basketball team.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the characteristics of an unbiased, representative sample: it must use random sampling from the entire target population (all high school students in the district).
2. Analyze the bias in each option:
   - A selects only AP students, who might study more than average.
   - C selects library users, who may study more or have different habits.
   - D selects athletes, who have specific schedule constraints.
3. Identify which option selects participants randomly from the entire population directory.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

Two datasets are represented in histograms. Both histograms show distributions with a mean of 50. Histogram X is tightly clustered, with almost all values between 45 and 55. Histogram Y is widely spread out, with values ranging from 20 to 80. Which of the following statements must be true?

A) The median of Dataset X is greater than the median of Dataset Y.

B) The standard deviation of Dataset X is greater than the standard deviation of Dataset Y.

C) The standard deviation of Dataset Y is greater than the standard deviation of Dataset X.

D) The range of Dataset X is equal to the range of Dataset Y.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall that standard deviation measures the spread or variability of a data set. A distribution that is widely spread out has a larger standard deviation than a distribution that is tightly clustered around the mean.
2. Compare the spread of Histogram X (45 to 55) and Histogram Y (20 to 80).
3. Identify which dataset has a larger spread and therefore a larger standard deviation.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium

The average (arithmetic mean) score of a group of students on a test is 85. The group consists of 15 boys, who had an average score of 82, and some number of girls, who had an average score of 90. How many girls are in the group?

A) 9

B) 10

C) 12

D) 15

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Let the number of girls in the group be `g`. The total number of students in the group is `15 + g`.
2. Set up an equation for the total sum of all test scores:
   - Total score = `Average score * Total students = 85 * (15 + g)`.
   - Sum of boys' scores = `15 * 82 = 1,230`.
   - Sum of girls' scores = `g * 90 = 90g`.
3. Set the total score equal to the sum of the boys' and girls' scores: `85(15 + g) = 1,230 + 90g`.
4. Expand the left side: `1,275 + 85g = 1,230 + 90g`.
5. Solve for `g`. Subtract `85g` from both sides and subtract 1,230 from both sides.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Hard

A polling agency conducted a survey to estimate the percentage of voters in a state who support a candidate. Based on a random sample of 1,000 voters, the candidate has 52% support with a 3% margin of error at a 95% confidence level. Which of the following statements is the most appropriate conclusion?

A) Exactly 52% of all voters in the state support the candidate.

B) There is a 95% probability that the candidate will win the election.

C) It is highly likely that the true percentage of all voters in the state who support the candidate is between 49% and 55%.

D) If the survey is repeated, the candidate will get 52% support 95% of the time.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Understand the statistical meaning of a confidence interval with a margin of error: it estimates that the true population value lies within the interval `Estimate - Margin of Error` to `Estimate + Margin of Error` with a certain confidence.
2. Calculate the interval: `52% - 3% = 49%` to `52% + 3% = 55%`.
3. A 95% confidence level means we are 95% confident that the true population percentage falls within this interval.
4. Match this statistical interpretation to the options. (Note: it does not mean the sample value will always be exactly 52%, nor does it guarantee winning).

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

A manufacturer claims that its light bulbs last an average of 1,000 hours. A consumer advocacy group tests a random sample of 50 of these light bulbs and finds a mean lifetime of 980 hours with a margin of error of 15 hours. Which of the following is the most appropriate conclusion based on these results?

A) The results support the manufacturer's claim because 980 is close to 1,000.

B) The results do not support the manufacturer's claim because the interval of plausible values (965 to 995 hours) does not include 1,000.

C) The results prove that every light bulb produced by the manufacturer lasts less than 1,000 hours.

D) The sample size of 50 is too small to make any conclusion.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Calculate the confidence interval of plausible values for the mean lifetime: `980 - 15 = 965` hours to `980 + 15 = 995` hours.
2. Determine if the manufacturer's claim of 1,000 hours falls inside this interval of plausible values.
3. Since 1,000 is outside the interval, evaluate if the result supports or contradicts the manufacturer's claim.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

The mean and standard deviation of a dataset of temperatures are 68 degrees Fahrenheit and 4 degrees Fahrenheit, respectively. If 3 degrees Fahrenheit is added to every temperature in the dataset, what will be the new mean and the new standard deviation?

A) New mean: 71, new standard deviation: 4

B) New mean: 71, new standard deviation: 7

C) New mean: 68, new standard deviation: 4

D) New mean: 68, new standard deviation: 7

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall how adding a constant value `c` to every number in a dataset affects the mean and the standard deviation:
   - **Effect on Mean:** The mean increases by the constant: `New Mean = Old Mean + c`.
   - **Effect on Standard Deviation:** The standard deviation measures the distance between data points. Adding the same value to all points shifts the entire distribution without changing the distances between points. Therefore, the standard deviation remains unchanged.
2. Substitute the values: `c = 3`, `Old Mean = 68`, and `Old SD = 4`.
3. Calculate the new mean and determine the new standard deviation.
