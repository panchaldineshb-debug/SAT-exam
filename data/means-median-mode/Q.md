### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Easy

A student has taken 4 exams and received scores of 85, 92, 88, and 95. What score must the student receive on a 5th exam to have an average (mean) score of exactly 90?

A) 90

B) 92

C) 94

D) 96

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall the definition of the mean: `mean = (sum of all scores) / (number of scores)`.
2. If the mean of 5 exams must be 90, multiply the target mean (90) by the total number of exams (5) to find the required sum of all 5 scores.
3. Calculate the sum of the 4 exams the student has already taken: `85 + 92 + 88 + 95`.
4. Subtract the sum of the first 4 exams from the required total sum of 5 exams. This difference is the score needed on the 5th exam.

### Question 2
**Difficulty:** Easy

The table below shows the distribution of the number of siblings for a class of 25 students.

| Number of Siblings | Number of Students (Frequency) |
| :--- | :--- |
| 0 | 4 |
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | 6 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 2 |

What is the median number of siblings for the 25 students?

A) 1

B) 1.5

C) 2

D) 2.5

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. The median is the middle value when all 25 data points are arranged in ascending order.
2. Since there are 25 data points (an odd number), the position of the median can be found using the formula: `(N + 1) / 2`, where `N` is the total number of students. Use this to find which sorted position represents the median.
3. List out or count the frequencies starting from 0 siblings:
   - The first 4 students have 0 siblings.
   - The next 10 students have 1 sibling.
4. Determine which number of siblings category contains the middle student's position.

### Question 3
**Difficulty:** Easy

A survey asked 15 customers to rate their satisfaction with a product on a scale from 1 to 5. The ratings received were:
3, 4, 5, 2, 4, 3, 5, 4, 1, 4, 3, 2, 4, 5, 2

What is the mode of the satisfaction ratings?

A) 2

B) 3

C) 4

D) 5

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Recall that the mode is the value that appears most frequently in a data set.
2. Count the number of times each rating (1, 2, 3, 4, and 5) appears in the given list.
3. The rating with the highest frequency (highest count) is the mode.

### Question 4
**Difficulty:** Medium

A set of 10 houses has a median price of \$300,000 and a mean price of \$320,000. An 11th house, priced at \$1,200,000, is added to the data set. Which of the following statements must be true about the new median and new mean of the 11 houses compared to the original 10 houses?

A) Both the mean and the median will increase by the same amount.

B) The mean will increase significantly, while the median will increase by a smaller amount or remain the same.

C) The median will increase significantly, while the mean will remain the same.

D) Both the mean and the median will decrease.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Consider the definition of the mean: it is the sum of all values divided by the total count. Adding an outlier that is much larger than the current mean (like \$1,200,000) will greatly increase the sum and therefore pull the mean upward.
2. Consider the definition of the median: it is the middle value of the sorted list.
3. Adding a single large value to the very high end of a sorted list of 10 values shifts the middle position from between the 5th and 6th values to the 6th value.
4. Compare how much the middle value (median) can shift versus how much the sum divided by the count (mean) shifts when a very large outlier is introduced.

### Question 5
**Difficulty:** Medium

The frequency table below shows the number of goals scored by a soccer team in each of their 20 games last season.

| Goals Scored | Number of Games (Frequency) |
| :--- | :--- |
| 0 | 3 |
| 1 | 7 |
| 2 | 5 |
| 3 | 4 |
| 4 | 1 |

What is the mean number of goals scored per game?

A) 1.50

B) 1.65

C) 1.80

D) 2.00

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. To calculate the mean from a frequency table, find the total number of goals scored across all games.
2. Multiply each number of goals by its corresponding frequency (number of games):
   - Goals from games with 0 goals: `0 * 3`
   - Goals from games with 1 goal: `1 * 7`
   - (continue for 2, 3, and 4 goals)
3. Add these products together to get the total number of goals.
4. Divide this total by the total number of games (20) to find the mean.

### Question 6
**Difficulty:** Medium

In a small company, the 8 entry-level employees each earn an average salary of \$45,000 per year. The 2 managers each earn an average salary of \$80,000 per year. What is the average (mean) salary of all 10 employees at the company?

A) \$52,000

B) \$53,500

C) \$62,500

D) \$71,000

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. This is a weighted average problem. You cannot simply average \$45,000 and \$80,000 because there is a different number of employees in each salary category.
2. Calculate the total annual salary of the 8 entry-level employees by multiplying `8` by `\$45,000`.
3. Calculate the total annual salary of the 2 managers by multiplying `2` by `\$80,000`.
4. Add the two total sums to find the combined annual salary for all 10 employees.
5. Divide the combined salary by the total number of employees (10) to find the overall mean.

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

A list of 5 positive integers consists of: 3, 6, x, 12, 15. The list is sorted in ascending order. If the mean of the list is equal to the median of the list, what is the value of x?

A) 6

B) 7

C) 8

D) 9

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Since the list `3, 6, x, 12, 15` is already sorted in ascending order and has 5 numbers (an odd count), the median is the middle value, which is `x`.
2. Write an algebraic expression for the mean of the 5 numbers: `(3 + 6 + x + 12 + 15) / 5`.
3. Simplify the sum of the constants in the numerator.
4. Set the expression for the mean equal to the median (`x`), forming the equation: `(36 + x) / 5 = x`.
5. Solve this equation for `x` by multiplying both sides by 5 and isolating `x`.

### Question 8
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard

The lists below show the daily high temperatures, in degrees Fahrenheit, for two cities over a 7-day period.

City A: 72, 73, 72, 74, 73, 72, 74
City B: 60, 68, 72, 75, 80, 85, 88

Which of the following statements is true about the standard deviations and ranges of the temperatures for the two cities?

A) The range of City A is greater, and the standard deviation of City A is greater.

B) The range of City B is greater, and the standard deviation of City B is greater.

C) The range of City A is greater, but the standard deviation of City B is greater.

D) The range of City B is greater, but the standard deviation of City A is greater.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Calculate the range for City A: find the difference between its maximum temperature and its minimum temperature.
2. Calculate the range for City B: find the difference between its maximum temperature and its minimum temperature. Compare the two ranges.
3. Recall that standard deviation measures the spread or variability of data points around their mean.
4. Look at how close the temperatures in City A are to each other (all are clustered tightly between 72 and 74).
5. Look at how spread out the temperatures in City B are (ranging from 60 to 88).
6. Determine which city has more variability (spread) and therefore a higher standard deviation.

### Question 9
**Difficulty:** Hard

A teacher's grading spreadsheet contains the test scores of 30 students. The mean score is 82 and the median score is 80. The teacher realizes that two students' scores were entered incorrectly: one student's score should be increased from 75 to 85, and another student's score should be increased from 60 to 80. How do these corrections affect the mean and median of the class?

A) The mean increases, and the median must increase.

B) The mean increases, and the median could increase or remain the same.

C) The mean remains the same, but the median increases.

D) Both the mean and the median remain the same.

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Determine the effect on the mean: The mean is calculated from the sum of all scores. Since two scores are being increased (one by 10 points and the other by 20 points), the total sum of all scores increases. What does this mean for the class average?
2. Determine the effect on the median: The original median score is 80, meaning the middle of the sorted list of 30 scores is 80.
3. Look at the original scores that were corrected: 75 and 60. Both of these scores are strictly below the original median of 80.
4. Look at the new scores: 75 becomes 85 (moving from below the median to above the median), and 60 becomes 80 (moving from below the median to equal to the median).
5. Since data points are shifting from below the median to the median or above it, the middle values in the sorted list might shift upwards, or they might stay the same if there are already multiple scores of 80 occupying the middle positions of the list.

### Question 10
**Difficulty:** Hard

A company has two branches: Branch A and Branch B.
- Branch A has 15 employees with a median salary of \$50,000.
- Branch B has 25 employees with a median salary of \$60,000.

What is the minimum possible number of employees in the combined company (both branches) who earn \$50,000 or more?

A) 8

B) 13

C) 21

D) 22

**Hint / Guided Steps:**
1. Analyze Branch A: It has 15 employees (an odd number). The median is \$50,000, which is the 8th employee's salary when sorted. This means at least 8 employees (the 8th through 15th in sorted order) must earn \$50,000 or more.
2. Analyze Branch B: It has 25 employees (an odd number). The median is \$60,000, which is the 13th employee's salary when sorted. This means at least 13 employees (the 13th through 25th in sorted order) must earn \$60,000 or more.
3. Since \$60,000 is greater than \$50,000, any employee who earns \$60,000 or more automatically earns \$50,000 or more.
4. Sum the minimum number of employees earning \$50,000 or more from Branch A and the minimum number of employees earning \$60,000 or more from Branch B.
