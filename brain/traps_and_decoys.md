# Traps & Decoys Catalog: Pedagogical Taxonomy

This catalog maintains the authoritative classification of College Board decoy options, tracking traps Student has fallen for versus traps Student has systematically defeated.

---

## 1. Catalog of Trap Classifications

### `intermediate_step_trap`
- **Definition:** The question requires multiple algebraic steps (e.g. solve for x, then compute 2x + 1 or x + y). The decoy choice is the raw value of the intermediate variable x.
- **Root Cause:** Target Check failure; rushing to select an answer as soon as the first variable is isolated.
- **Status:** Defeated on Systems of Equations (Flawless on August 17 drill); vigilance maintained for Quadratics.

### `sign_flip_trap`
- **Definition:** Dropping or flipping a negative sign during factoring, distribution, or substitution (e.g., getting (x - 3)(x + 4) instead of (x + 3)(x - 4), or flipping signs when isolating variables across an inequality).
- **Root Cause:** Mental math fatigue and lack of scratch-paper verification under time constraints.
- **Status:** Active Watchlist. Mastered in linear ratio equations; monitor closely under Module 2 fatigue.

### `rushing_review_buffer_trap`
- **Definition:** Finishing a section or drill with time remaining, but immediately submitting rather than enforcing the review buffer. Results in unforced errors on the last 5 questions.
- **Root Cause:** False sense of security and test fatigue.
- **Status:** Strict Protocol Enforced. Mandatory 5-minute review buffer dedicated to the 5 hardest questions.

### `poison_word_trap` / `extreme_modifier_trap`
- **Definition:** Reading comprehension decoys containing absolute, aggressive modifiers (*never*, *always*, *entirely*, *completely*, *impossible*, *solely*, *all*, *none*, *exclusively*, *inevitably*).
- **Root Cause:** Instinctive attraction to strong statements rather than academically defensible, qualified conclusions.
- **Status:** Defeated. Flawless 10/10 milestone achieved on Reading Mastery Sprint via the automatic Poison Word Scan.

### `premature_rounding_trap`
- **Definition:** Clearing the calculator or rounding intermediate decimals before arriving at the final numerical answer, especially in compound interest and percentage grid-ins.
- **Root Cause:** Incomplete Desmos syntax; failure to use the Desmos One-Line Entry Rule.
- **Status:** Active Watchlist. Student must input complete expressions into a single line in Desmos without intermediate rounding.

### `negative_sign_ratio_trap`
- **Definition:** In standard form linear systems a1/a2 = b1/b2, failing to preserve double negatives (e.g., -6/15 = k/-25 leading to k = 10).
- **Root Cause:** Rushing through ratio equivalencies.
- **Status:** Defeated. Successfully executed on August 4 micro-drill.

### `margin_of_error_trap`
- **Definition:** Applying margin of error directly to the total population rather than adding/subtracting it from the survey sample percentage first before multiplying by population.
- **Root Cause:** Formula misapplication.
- **Status:** Defeated. 100% precision on population sanity check drills.

### `growth_rate_trap`
- **Definition:** Interpreting an exponential multiplier such as (1.32) or (1.45) as a flat addition (+32 or +45 units) instead of a percentage growth (32% or 45% increase).
- **Root Cause:** Misconception between linear addition and exponential multiplication.
- **Status:** Defeated. Conceptual blindspot officially closed on August 17 mega-drill.
