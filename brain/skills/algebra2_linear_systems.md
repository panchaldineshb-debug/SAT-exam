## Step-by-Step Lesson Breakdown (Foundations / Medium / Advanced)

### Foundations (Easy): Direct Substitution
- **Core Concept:** When one variable is already isolated (e.g., y = 2x - 1) or a sub-expression is given (e.g., x + 7 = 10), substitute directly into the second equation.
- **Protocol:**
  1. Substitute the isolated expression into the second equation.
  2. Solve for the remaining variable.
  3. Execute the Target Check: Verify what the question asks for (x, y, or an ordered pair).

### Medium: Elimination & Coefficient Matching
- **Core Concept:** Standard form Ax + By = C. Multiply one or both equations to eliminate a variable.
- **No Solution vs. Infinitely Many Solutions:**
  - No Solution: Slopes are equal, y-intercepts differ (A1/A2 = B1/B2 != C1/C2).
  - Infinitely Many Solutions: Identical lines (A1/A2 = B1/B2 = C1/C2).
- **Protocol:** Align coefficients on scratch paper before adding or subtracting. Double-check sign distributions when multiplying through by negative numbers.

### Advanced (Hard): Nonlinear Systems & Parameter Constraints
- **Core Concept:** A linear equation intersecting a quadratic equation (y = mx + b and y = ax^2 + bx + c).
- **Discriminant Analysis:** Set mx + b = ax^2 + bx + c, rearrange to standard form, and evaluate b^2 - 4ac:
  - b^2 - 4ac > 0: 2 real solutions (2 intersections).
  - b^2 - 4ac = 0: Exactly 1 real solution (tangent line).
  - b^2 - 4ac < 0: 0 real solutions (no intersection).

## Prescribed Micro-Drill Routines
1. **Target Check Verification:** Always write the target quantity in a box in the upper right corner of scratch paper before solving.
2. **Double-Verification for Grid-ins:** For any grid-in system result, plug x and y back into both original equations to confirm equality before submitting.
3. **5-Minute Parameter Drill:** 3 questions testing k-parameter constraints for "no solution" and "infinitely many solutions" once per week.
