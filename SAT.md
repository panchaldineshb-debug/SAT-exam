What the SAT actually includes now (2026)
The test has two scored sections, each split into two adaptive modules:
1. Reading and Writing (RW)
•	54 questions total (27 per module)
•	64 minutes total (32 min per module)
•	All multiple-choice (4 answer options)
•	Short passages (25–150 words), one question per passage
•	Content domains:
•	Craft and Structure (~28%)
•	Information and Ideas (~26%)
•	Standard English Conventions (~26%)
•	Expression of Ideas (~20%)[careerlauncher +2]
2. Math
•	44 questions total (22 per module)
•	70 minutes total (35 min per module)
•	~75% multiple-choice, ~25% student-produced responses (you type in a number)
•	Calculator allowed on the entire section (Desmos built into the Bluebook app)
•	Content areas:
•	Algebra (~35%)
•	Advanced Math (~35%)
•	Problem-Solving & Data Analysis (~15%)
•	Geometry & Trigonometry (~15%)[shiksha +3]
